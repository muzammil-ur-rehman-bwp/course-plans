# Lab Assignment 14 — Position Paper: Open Problem (Graded)

**Weight:** part of the weekly Lab Work component (10% of course grade, averaged across all labs).

## Deliverable
Submit `lab14_position_paper.md` (or `.pdf`) containing Tasks A–D from `lab-manuals/lab-14.md`.

## Grading Rubric
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Task A complete (open question restated precisely, genuinely in the student's own words) | 2 |
| Task B complete (established-vs-open distinction made clearly and specifically) | 2 |
| Task C complete (500–700-word position paper proposing a specific, well-argued sub-question) | 4 |
| Task D complete (capstone connection assessed honestly and specifically) | 2 |
| **Total** | **10** |

## Notes
- There is no requirement that the proposed sub-question (Task C) must become the student's actual
  capstone topic; Task D is graded on honesty and specificity of the assessment, not on reaching
  any particular conclusion.
- A near-verbatim restatement of the handout in Task A cannot receive full credit.
