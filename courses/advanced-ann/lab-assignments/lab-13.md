# Lab Assignment 13 — Research Methods Workshop (Graded)

**Weight:** part of the weekly Lab Work component (10% of course grade, averaged across all labs).
Directly feeds the Research Proposal Capstone (see `assignments/capstone-proposal-guidelines.md`)
but is graded separately as this week's lab.

## Deliverable
Submit `lab13_research_methods.md` (or `.pdf`) containing Tasks A–D from `lab-manuals/lab-13.md`.

## Grading Rubric
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Task A complete (paper excerpt critiqued using all four Week 13 §1 questions) | 2 |
| Task B complete (problem statement drafted, precision-tested, and revised with both versions shown) | 3 |
| Task C complete (peer precision-test feedback given and received, both recorded) | 2 |
| Task D complete (5+ papers annotated with claim/finding and at least one relating sentence each) | 3 |
| **Total** | **10** |

## Notes
- This lab's content is expected to directly seed the Week 8 problem-statement check-in and the
  capstone's eventual related-work survey; it is graded on rigor of process here, not on how
  polished the final capstone topic turns out to be.
- A survey (Task D) with five accurate but entirely isolated summaries cannot receive full credit,
  per the Week 13 §3 standard.
