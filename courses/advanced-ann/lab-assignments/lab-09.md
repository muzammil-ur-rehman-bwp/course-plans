# Lab Assignment 9 — Position Paper: Statistical-Physics Analogies (Graded)

**Weight:** part of the weekly Lab Work component (10% of course grade, averaged across all labs).

## Deliverable
Submit `lab09_position_paper.md` (or `.pdf`) containing Tasks A–D from `lab-manuals/lab-09.md`.

## Grading Rubric
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Task A complete (one claim restated precisely, in the student's own words) | 2 |
| Task B complete (the claim's idealized assumptions specifically identified) | 2 |
| Task C complete (500–700-word position paper reaching a defensible, non-overstated conclusion, citing Week 9 §4 specifically) | 4 |
| Task D complete (a specific peer counterpoint identified and a substantive rebuttal-response written) | 2 |
| **Total** | **10** |

## Notes
- The confidence level argued for does not need to match any "official" answer — it is graded on
  how specifically it engages the stated limits and how well it is defended, not on landing at a
  particular point on the confidence spectrum.
- A paper that asserts either extreme ("fully proven" or "completely irrelevant") without engaging
  Week 9 §4's specific limits cannot receive full credit on Task C.
