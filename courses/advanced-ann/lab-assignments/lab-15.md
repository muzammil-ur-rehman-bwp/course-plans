# Lab Assignment 15 — Peer-Review Workshop and Revision Plan (Graded)

**Weight:** part of the weekly Lab Work component (10% of course grade, averaged across all labs).
Distinct from, and in addition to, the Research Proposal Capstone's own Week 15 draft-proposal
deadline (see `assignments/capstone-rubric.md`).

## Deliverable
Submit `lab15_peer_review_and_revision_plan.md` (or `.pdf`) containing Tasks A–D from
`lab-manuals/lab-15.md`.

## Grading Rubric
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Task A complete (structured feedback given to a peer and received feedback recorded in full) | 3 |
| Task B complete (received feedback summarized clearly, keyed to all four components) | 2 |
| Task C complete (a specific response — action or justified disagreement — given for each item) | 3 |
| Task D complete (a clear, appropriately specific disclosure statement drafted) | 2 |
| **Total** | **10** |

## Notes
- This lab is graded on the rigor and specificity of the review process, not on the quality of the
  underlying capstone topic itself (that is graded separately, per `assignments/capstone-
  rubric.md`).
- Disclosed peer collaboration at this stage is expected and required to be disclosed (Task D), not
  penalized; see `course-plan.md` §10.
