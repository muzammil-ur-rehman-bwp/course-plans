# Lab Assignment 7 — Position Paper: Grokking Hypotheses (Graded)

**Weight:** part of the weekly Lab Work component (10% of course grade, averaged across all labs).

## Deliverable
Submit `lab07_position_paper.md` (or `.pdf`) containing Tasks A–D from `lab-manuals/lab-07.md`.

## Grading Rubric
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Task A complete (three-phase curve restated precisely, in the student's own words) | 2 |
| Task B complete (both hypotheses restated precisely, with their cited evidence) | 2 |
| Task C complete (a genuinely new, specific distinguishing piece of evidence proposed and argued) | 4 |
| Task D complete (a specific peer counterpoint identified and a substantive rebuttal-response written) | 2 |
| **Total** | **10** |

## Notes
- Task C is graded on the precision and genuine novelty of the proposed distinguishing evidence,
  not on which hypothesis the student ultimately favors.
- A paper that only summarizes the lecture content without proposing new distinguishing evidence
  cannot receive full credit on Task C, regardless of writing quality.
