# Week 7 Summary — The Grokking Phenomenon

**Key takeaways:**
- Grokking (Power et al.) is a three-phase curve: rapid training-accuracy saturation, a long
  chance-level test-accuracy plateau, then a sharp, delayed rise to high test accuracy.
- It is theoretically puzzling because training loss gives no visible signal of whatever changes
  during the plateau to eventually produce the sharp transition.
- Two competing hypotheses — slow implicit-regularization-driven solution transition, and circuit
  formation between a memorizing and a generalizing internal strategy — are both consistent with
  the curve and not mutually exclusive; neither is currently the single settled explanation.

**You should now be able to:** state the grokking phenomenon and both competing hypotheses
precisely; identify what evidence currently does and does not establish; avoid overstating either
hypothesis as settled fact.
