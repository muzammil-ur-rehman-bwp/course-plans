# Week 1 Summary — The Neural-Network-Theory Research Frontier

**Key takeaways:**
- This course assumes the entire graduate *Artificial Neural Network* course as settled
  background and does not re-teach any of it; a self-assessment checklist is the Week 1 diagnostic.
- Several graduate-course topics (NTK, the Lottery Ticket Hypothesis) are revisited here not as
  repetition but as research-depth treatments — deriving mechanisms and surveying current critiques
  the graduate survey-level treatment did not have room for.
- A research proposal has four components — problem statement, related-work survey, proposed
  approach, feasibility argument — previewed now and treated in full in Week 13; the capstone (40%
  of the grade) is judged on the soundness of this plan, not on finished work.

**You should now be able to:** state which graduate-course topics this course assumes without
re-deriving them; map this course's topics onto the broader research landscape; outline a research
proposal's four required components.

**Next week:** The Neural Tangent Kernel revisited in depth — the infinite-width-limit derivation
sketch, the lazy-training regime, and a sharper critique of NTK's limits.
