# Week 16 Summary — Capstone Presentations and Course Review

**Key takeaways:**
- The course map, recapped: infinite-width pictures and their limits (NTK, mean-field, implicit
  bias, feature learning) → why training finds the solutions it finds (sharpness/SAM, grokking,
  scaling laws, statistical physics) → generalization and landscape geometry rigorously (double
  descent, PAC-Bayes, mode connectivity/LTH revisited) → doing research (methods, open problems,
  proposal work) → the capstone defense itself.

  theoretical ML venues (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR), are now unlocked as where this material leads next.
- The capstone defense is evaluated as a thesis-proposal defense: the soundness of the research
  plan matters more than whether anything was run, and honestly stating what remains unsettled is
  a strength, not a weakness.

**You should now be able to:** situate every course topic within the overall map; defend an
original research proposal under committee-style questioning; state where this course's material
leads in the broader postgraduate curriculum and current research literature.
