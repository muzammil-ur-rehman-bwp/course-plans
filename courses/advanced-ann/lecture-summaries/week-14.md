# Week 14 Summary — Current Open Problems Survey

**Key takeaways:**
- Three grounded current open problems surveyed: a practically predictive theory of feature
  learning's benefits; generalization bounds that are simultaneously tight, reparameterization-
  invariant, and computable; whether grokking and double descent share a common mechanism.
- This survey is explicitly flagged as fast-moving — the specific state of "open-ness" may date
  quickly, and the instructor refreshes the question set each offering.
- The discipline of stating precisely what is established versus what remains open, without
  collapsing to "nothing is known" or "this is solved," is the same discipline a sound capstone
  problem statement requires.

**You should now be able to:** state each surveyed open question precisely; distinguish what
current partial answers establish from what they are sometimes informally taken to establish;
identify a concrete, falsifiable sub-question that could seed a capstone topic.
