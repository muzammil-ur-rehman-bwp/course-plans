# Week 15 Summary — Research Proposal Work Session

**Key takeaways:**
- This week applies Week 13's precision test, survey-synthesis standard, and feasibility-argument
  standard to students' own complete draft proposals, via structured thesis-committee-style peer
  review.
- Useful feedback is specific and actionable, keyed to each of the four required proposal
  components, mirroring the capstone rubric's weighting.
- Receiving feedback well means evaluating it on its merits and producing a written revision plan,
  not dismissing it or accepting it uncritically; disclosed peer collaboration is expected, not a
  violation.

**You should now be able to:** apply the precision test and survey-synthesis standard to someone
else's draft proposal; give specific, actionable feedback; produce a written revision plan in
response to feedback received.

**Next week:** Capstone research proposal presentations and course review.
