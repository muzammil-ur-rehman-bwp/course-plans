# Lab Manual 15 — Structured Peer-Review Workshop and Revision Plan

**Duration:** 3 hours (peer-review workshop) | **Prerequisite:** Week 15 lecture; a complete draft
capstone proposal (all four required components) brought to the session

## Objectives
Give and receive structured, thesis-committee-style peer review on complete draft capstone
proposals, and produce a written revision plan in response to the feedback received.

## Setup
No code environment is required beyond what your own capstone topic needs. Bring your complete
draft proposal and the peer-review structured feedback form. Create a single document
`lab15_peer_review_and_revision_plan.md` (or `.pdf`).

## Procedure
1. **Task A — Review received:** exchange your draft proposal with a peer (or in a small group, if
   the instructor organizes it that way). Using the structured feedback form, give feedback on
   your peer's problem statement (precision test), related-work survey (synthesis standard),
   proposed approach (distinguishability from any single surveyed paper), and feasibility
   argument (named risk). Record the written feedback you receive on your own draft.
2. **Task B — Feedback summary:** summarize the specific, actionable feedback you received, keyed
   to each of the four components.
3. **Task C — Revision plan:** for each piece of feedback summarized in Task B, state whether and
   how you will address it in your final submission, or, if you disagree, state specifically why.
4. **Task D — Disclosure statement:** draft the acknowledgments paragraph you will include in your
   final proposal disclosing this peer-review collaboration, per `course-plan.md` §10.

## Expected Output
A single document containing Tasks A–D as clearly labeled sections, including the feedback you
received (Task A/B) and your complete revision plan (Task C).

## Submission
Submit `lab15_peer_review_and_revision_plan.md` (or `.pdf`) via the course submission system by
the end of the workshop session. **Paper Critique & Presentation due. Draft capstone proposal due**
(see `assignments/capstone-proposal-guidelines.md`).
