# Lab Manual 7 — Position Paper: Competing Hypotheses for Grokking

**Duration:** 3 hours (critical-writing/discussion format) | **Prerequisite:** Week 7 lecture

## Objectives
Precisely restate the grokking phenomenon and its two competing hypotheses, and produce a
reasoned, evaluated position on what evidence would favor one hypothesis over the other.

## Setup
No code environment is required this week. Bring the Week 7 handout (Power et al.'s central
observation and each hypothesis's claim and evidence, summarized). Create a single document
`lab07_position_paper.md` (or `.pdf`). An optional, ungraded small modular-arithmetic reproduction
is suggested for students who want to see the three-phase curve directly, but is not required.

## Procedure
1. **Task A — Phenomenon restatement:** in your own words, restate the three-phase grokking curve
   precisely enough that a reader unfamiliar with Week 7 could follow your argument from your
   restatement alone.
2. **Task B — Hypothesis restatement:** restate both competing hypotheses (slow implicit-
   regularization-driven transition; circuit formation) precisely, including what evidence each
   cites.
3. **Task C — Position paper draft (500–700 words):** argue which single additional piece of
   evidence (not already covered in lecture) would most clearly distinguish between the two
   hypotheses if it were observed, and why — citing the Week 7 lecture content's §3/§4 precisely.
4. **Task D — Peer exchange and rebuttal:** exchange drafts with a peer. Each of you identifies
   one point in the other's position where the opposite argument could reasonably be made. Append
   a 3–5 sentence rebuttal-response to your own paper addressing the counterpoint you received.

## Expected Output
A single document containing Tasks A–D as clearly labeled sections, ending with the Task D
rebuttal-response appended to the Task C draft.

## Submission
Submit `lab07_position_paper.md` (or `.pdf`) via the course submission system by the end of the
lab session.
