# Lab Manual 14 — Position Paper: A Current Open Problem

**Duration:** 3 hours (critical-writing/discussion format) | **Prerequisite:** Week 14 lecture

## Objectives
Precisely state one current open problem in neural network theory, what is established versus
open about it, and one concrete, falsifiable sub-question that could seed a capstone topic.

## Setup
No code environment is required this week. Bring the Week 14 handout (the three surveyed open
questions, or an instructor-approved alternative). Create a single document
`lab14_position_paper.md` (or `.pdf`).

## Procedure
1. **Task A — Question selection and restatement:** choose one of the three Week 14 open
   questions (or, with instructor approval, a different current open question connected to this
   course's material) and restate it precisely, in your own words.
2. **Task B — Established-vs-open analysis:** state specifically what is currently established
   about your chosen question (the restricted/toy cases where it is answered) and what remains
   open (the gap to a general, practically useful answer), following the Week 14 §4 discipline.
3. **Task C — Position paper draft (500–700 words):** argue what you consider the most promising
   current partial answer or research direction for your chosen question, being explicit about
   its own limits, and propose one concrete, falsifiable sub-question from it.
4. **Task D — Capstone connection:** in 100–150 words, state whether this sub-question is a
   plausible candidate for your own capstone topic, or if not, why not, connecting it explicitly
   to your Week 13 problem-statement work.

## Expected Output
A single document containing Tasks A–D as clearly labeled sections.

## Submission
Submit `lab14_position_paper.md` (or `.pdf`) via the course submission system by the end of the
lab session.
