# Quiz 6 — Open Problems Survey & Research Methods (Week 14)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State one of the three Week 14 open problems precisely, including what is established and what
   remains open about it. (2 pts)
2. A student's draft problem statement reads "I want to study scaling laws." Explain, in 1–2
   sentences, why this fails the Week 13 precision test, and briefly rewrite it so it passes. (2 pts)
3. Name the three things a postgraduate-level related-work survey must do that a weaker survey
   often fails at. (2 pts)
4. Name the three things a rigorous feasibility argument must state explicitly. (2 pts)
5. Explain why "this is a fast-moving area, so the specific open questions may date quickly" is a
   stated limitation of Week 14's survey rather than a reason to dismiss the survey's value. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
