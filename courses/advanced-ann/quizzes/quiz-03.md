# Quiz 3 — Scaling Laws (Week 8)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Write the empirical power-law form relating test loss to model size, and state what fitting a
   line in log-log space recovers. (2 pts)
2. State what the compute-optimal refinement (broadly attributed to Hoffmann et al.) corrects
   about an earlier, naive reading of scaling-law results. (2 pts)
3. Name one theoretical attempt to explain why scaling laws hold, and state what it does and does
   not fully explain. (2 pts)
4. Explain how the NTK/kernel-regime connection (§3 of Week 8's content) links scaling-law theory
   back to this course's own Week 2 material. (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "Since scaling laws are so empirically robust, we must already understand
   why they hold." Explain what is wrong with this claim. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
