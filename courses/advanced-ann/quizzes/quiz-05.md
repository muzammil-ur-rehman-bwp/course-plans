# Quiz 5 — Mode Connectivity & The Lottery Ticket Hypothesis Revisited (Week 12)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State what a high linear-interpolation loss barrier between two minima does and does not imply
   about the landscape. (2 pts)
2. Describe, in one or two sentences, how a bend-point (Bezier) path can achieve a lower loss
   along its whole length than the linear path between the same two minima. (2 pts)
3. State the linear-mode-connectivity refinement of the Lottery Ticket Hypothesis precisely. (2 pts)
4. Name one current critique of the Lottery Ticket Hypothesis's original claim at scale. (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "The linear-mode-connectivity refinement proves winning tickets are
   'already there' at initialization." Explain why this overstates what the refinement
   establishes, referencing the open question from Week 12 §4. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
