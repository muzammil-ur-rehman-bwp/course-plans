# Quiz 2 — Sharpness, Generalization, and SAM (Week 6)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the flat-vs-sharp-minima intuition for why flatness should predict better
   generalization. (2 pts)
2. Name two ways sharpness is measured in practice, and state one advantage of the
   perturbation-based proxy over the Hessian-top-eigenvalue estimate. (2 pts)
3. Explain, using ReLU's positive homogeneity, why a function-preserving reparameterization can
   change measured sharpness. (2 pts)
4. Write SAM's min-max objective and its first-order approximation for the worst-case
   perturbation $\hat\epsilon(w)$. (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "SAM's empirical success proves flat minima cause better generalization."
   State a more defensible conclusion SAM's results actually support. (2 pts)

before the quiz).**
