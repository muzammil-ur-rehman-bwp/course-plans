# Quiz 4 — Double Descent Revisited (Week 10)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Define the interpolation threshold, and state why it organizes double descent across all three
   axes. (2 pts)
2. Name the three axes along which double descent has been rigorously characterized (Nakkiran et
   al.), and give one concrete example setting for each. (2 pts)
3. Explain why test error can transiently worsen as training-set size $n$ grows, near a
   sample-size-dependent interpolation threshold. (2 pts)
4. State what the random-matrix-theoretic explanation for double descent robustly establishes, and
   what it is considerably less complete about. (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "More training data can never hurt test performance." Using this week's
   material, explain precisely when this claim is false. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
