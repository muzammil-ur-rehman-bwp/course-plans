# Week 15 Lecture Plan — Advanced Artificial Neural Network (Post Graduate)
## Topic: Research Proposal Work Session

**Duration:** 2 hours structured work session + 3 hour peer-review workshop

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Apply the Week 13 precision test and survey-synthesis standard to a peer's complete draft
   proposal. (*Apply*)
2. Produce specific, actionable feedback and a written revision plan in response to feedback
   received. (*Create*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Framing | What a thesis-committee-style peer review looks for, recapped |
| 0:20–1:20 | Draft exchange & review | Students exchange complete draft proposals; apply precision test, survey-synthesis standard, and feasibility-argument standard |
| 1:20–1:50 | Feedback delivery | Structured, specific feedback given in writing, keyed to each of the four required components |
| 1:50–2:00 | Revision planning | Each student begins a written revision plan |

### Materials/Equipment
- Peer-review structured feedback form (keyed to `assignments/capstone-rubric.md`'s four
  components)
- Students' own complete draft proposals

### Formative Check (in-class)
Give one peer's problem statement the precision test out loud and state, specifically, what
evidence would or would not resolve it as written.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 15: structured peer-review workshop and written revision plan (see `lab-manuals/lab-15.md`).
**Paper Critique & Presentation due. Draft capstone proposal due** (all four required components).
