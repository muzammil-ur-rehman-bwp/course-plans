# Quiz 1 — Advanced CNN Architectures (Week 2)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the ResNet residual formulation $H(x) = \ldots$ and explain in one sentence why it makes
   learning an identity mapping easy. (2 pts)
2. What distinguishes DenseNet's dense connectivity from ResNet's skip connection — addition or
   concatenation? (2 pts)
3. Write the cost formula for a standard convolution and for a depthwise separable convolution,
   and state the approximate reduction factor. (2 pts)
4. Why does adding self-loops (the $+I$ in $\tilde A = A+I$) matter when later discussing GNNs —
   name the analogous CNN-architecture concept from this week that it parallels. (2 pts)
5. In a depthwise separable convolution, which stage (depthwise or pointwise) is responsible for
   mixing information across channels? (2 pts)

before the quiz).**
