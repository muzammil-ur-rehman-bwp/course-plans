# Quiz 2 — The Transformer From Scratch (Week 4)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Write the scaled dot-product attention formula and explain why the $1/\sqrt{d_k}$ scaling is
   necessary. (2 pts)
2. In multi-head attention with $d_{model}=512$ and $h=8$ heads, what is the per-head dimension
   $d_k$? (2 pts)
3. State the sinusoidal positional encoding formula for $PE_{(pos,2i)}$. (2 pts)
4. What attention-masking pattern distinguishes a decoder-only (GPT-style) model from an
   encoder-only (BERT-style) model? (2 pts)
5. In a Vision Transformer, what role does the `Conv2d(kernel_size=stride=patch_size)` operation
   play? (2 pts)

before the quiz).**
