# Week 14 Lecture Plan — Deep Learning (Graduate)
## Topic: Research Methods in Deep Learning; Capstone Work Time

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour lab/seminar

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Apply a structured framework for reading and critiquing a deep learning paper. (*Apply*)
2. Analyze reproducibility challenges specific to deep learning (compute cost, hyperparameter
   sensitivity, benchmark culture). (*Analyze*)
3. Produce a finalized capstone topic and an initial literature list. (*Apply*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Reading a paper efficiently | Abstract → figures/results → method → related work → full read |
| 0:20–0:50 | Critiquing a paper | Claim, evidence, baseline adequacy, ablations |
| 0:50–1:15 | DL-specific reproducibility | Compute cost as a reproduction barrier; hyperparameter sensitivity; benchmark-culture critiques |
| 1:15–1:25 | Break | — |
| 1:25–2:00 | Structured in-class critique exercise | Small groups critique a provided short paper excerpt |

### Materials/Equipment
- Paper-critique handout and checklist
- A short paper excerpt for the in-class exercise

### Formative Check (in-class)
Each group reports the excerpt's central claim, one piece of supporting/unsupporting evidence,
and one reproducibility concern (compute, hyperparameters, or benchmark culture).

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 14: Structured capstone work session — finalize topic, assemble a 3–5 paper candidate
reading list, and draft the proposal's motivating question (see `lab-manuals/lab-14.md`).
