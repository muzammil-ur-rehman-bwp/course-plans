# Week 1 Summary — Graduate Deep Learning Overview

**Key takeaways:**
- This course assumes the undergraduate DL survey (CNN/RNN/attention/Transformer-survey/VAE/GAN)
  and the tabular MDP/RL foundation from *Artificial Intelligence*, Graduate; neither is
  re-taught.
- This course does not duplicate *Artificial Neural Network*, Graduate's theory/training-dynamics
  focus — it is architecture-and-technique focused.
- The semester's map: advanced CNNs → the Transformer from scratch and its variants →
  self-supervised learning → diffusion models → graph neural networks → deep RL → large-scale
  training → multimodal models → research methods and capstone.

**You should now be able to:** state what this course assumes vs. what it adds depth to; set up
the PyTorch/torchvision/`torch_geometric` environment; map a given topic to its week.

**Next week:** advanced CNN architectures — the ResNet residual formulation in depth, DenseNet,
and efficiency-focused depthwise-separable convolutions.
