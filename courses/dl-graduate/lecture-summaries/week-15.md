# Week 15 Summary — Research Project Work Session

**Key takeaways:**
- A literature review must end with an explicit, stated gap or question — not just a list of
  summaries.
- A reproduced experiment should state clearly what was scaled down from the original and why;
  an extended experiment should hold all factors fixed except the one varied, with multiple
  seeds reported for any stochastic method.
- Structured peer feedback on a practice talk addresses problem clarity, experiment soundness,
  and honesty about limitations.

**You should now be able to:** finalize a literature review with a stated gap, design a fair
reproduced/extended experiment, and give/receive structured feedback on a research talk.
