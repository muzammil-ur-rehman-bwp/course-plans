# Week 14 Summary — Research Methods in Deep Learning; Capstone Work Time

**Key takeaways:**
- Read a paper efficiently: abstract → figures/results → method → related work → full read.
- Critique a paper on claim, evidence, baseline fairness, ablations, and reproducibility.
- Deep learning raises specific reproducibility challenges beyond general research-methods
  concerns: compute cost as a reproduction barrier, hyperparameter sensitivity, and
  benchmark-culture critiques (leaderboard-chasing, contamination, benchmark-vs-robustness gaps).

**You should now be able to:** apply a structured critique checklist to a DL paper excerpt, and
identify a finalized capstone topic with an initial 3–5 paper candidate reading list.

**Next week:** research project work session — literature review and experiment work time, and
the Paper Critique & Presentation in-class talks.
