# Quiz 4 — 2D Arrays, Strings, Objects/References (Week 8)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Given `int[][] grid = {{1,2,3},{4,5,6}};`, what is `grid[1][0]`? (1 pt)
2. Give one concrete reason why `==` should not be used to compare two `String`s for content
   equality. (2 pts)
3. Given `int[] a = {1, 2, 3}; int[] b = a;`, what does `b[0] = 99;` do to `a[0]`, and why? (2 pts)
4. What is `null`, and what specific exception is thrown when you try to call a method or access
   a field through a `null` reference? (3 pts)
5. What is wrong with this code?
   ```java
   int[] values;
   System.out.println(values.length);
   ```
   (2 pts)
