# Lab Manual 15 — Proposal Workshop and Revision Plan

**Duration:** 3 hours | **Prerequisite:** Week 15 lecture; a complete draft research proposal

**Format note:** this is a non-code, workshop lab — there is no notebook/implementation
component this week.

## Objectives
Participate in two committee-style peer-review rotations on draft capstone proposals; produce a
concrete, specific written revision plan.

## Setup
1. Submit your complete draft proposal (all four required components) before the session, per
   `assignments/capstone-proposal-guidelines.md`.
2. Bring the structured peer-feedback form (problem statement / survey / approach / feasibility).

## Procedure
1. **Task A — Rotation 1 (reviewer):** review an assigned peer's draft proposal; pose at least
   one specific question targeting the problem statement's precision and at least one targeting
   the related-work survey's **synthesis** (not just its accuracy).
2. **Task B — Rotation 1 (presenter):** receive feedback on your own draft from a different
   reviewer; take specific notes on each point raised.
3. **Task C — Rotation 2:** repeat Tasks A and B with a new reviewer/presenter pairing.
4. **Task D — Revision plan:** immediately after Rotation 2, write a revision plan: for each
   specific piece of feedback received across both rotations, state exactly what will change in
   your proposal before Week 16, or a specific, defensible reason why not.

## Expected Output
A written revision plan (Task D) referencing specific feedback from both rotations, submitted
alongside brief notes from your own Task A/C reviewer role (the questions you posed to your
assigned peers).

## Submission
Submit your revision plan and reviewer notes via the course submission system by the end of the
lab session.
