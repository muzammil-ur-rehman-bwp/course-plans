# Quiz 2 — Many-Valued/Paraconsistent Logics & SROIQ (Weeks 3–4)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Compute `U → U` under Kleene's K3 and under Łukasiewicz's Ł3, and state both results. (2 pts)
2. In the Belnap–Dunn FDE logic, what does the truth value **B** represent, and why does
   `P ∧ ¬P` evaluating to B not force an unrelated atom Q to any particular value? (2 pts)
3. Name two SROIQ constructs beyond ALC's ⊓, ⊔, ¬, ∃R.C, ∀R.C, and give one example axiom using
   each. (2 pts)
4. State the complexity class of SROIQ concept satisfiability, and name the two constructs
   whose interaction is primarily responsible for the jump beyond SHIQ's EXPTIME-completeness.
   (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "Dropping the regularity condition on SROIQ's role inclusion axioms just
   makes reasoning slower." Identify what is wrong with this claim. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
