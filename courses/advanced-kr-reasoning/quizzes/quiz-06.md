# Quiz 6 — Explanation/Justification Research & Ontology Evolution (Weeks 11–12)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Define a justification for an entailment, and explain why minimality is required. (2 pts)
2. State the difference between a black-box and a glass-box justification-finding method. (2 pts)
3. True or false, with one sentence of justification: an entailment can have only one
   justification. (2 pts)
4. State one reason a purely syntactic diff between two ontology versions can be misleading about
   the actual semantic change between them. (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "A backward-compatible ontology update is always better than one that
   loses an entailment." Identify what is wrong with this claim. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
