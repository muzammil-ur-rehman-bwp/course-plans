# Quiz 4 — Neuro-Symbolic Integration & Formal Verification (Weeks 7–9)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Write the product t-norm and product t-conorm formulas, and state why the product t-norm is
   generally preferred over the Gödel t-norm for gradient-based learning. (2 pts)
2. State one thing gained and one thing lost when neural theorem proving replaces exact
   unification with soft, embedding-similarity-based unification. (2 pts)
3. State the model-checking problem `M ⊨ φ` precisely for a finite Kripke structure M and an LTL
   formula φ. (2 pts)
4. In the automata-theoretic approach to LTL model checking, what does a reachable accepting
   cycle in the product `M ⊗ A_¬φ` indicate? (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "LTL model checking is PSPACE-complete, so it's always impractical for
   any real system." Identify what is wrong with this claim. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
