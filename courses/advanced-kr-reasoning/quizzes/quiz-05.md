# Quiz 5 — Multi-Agent Belief Merging (Week 10)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the key structural difference between AGM belief revision and multi-agent belief
   merging. (2 pts)
2. Define a distance-based merging operator's selection rule in terms of `Mod(IC)` and aggregate
   distance to a profile. (2 pts)
3. State postulate IC2 (commutativity) for a belief-merging operator, and explain in one sentence
   why it would be irrational for a merging operator to violate it. (2 pts)
4. True or false, with one sentence of justification: sum aggregation and max aggregation always
   produce the same merged result. (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "Belief merging is just AGM revision applied one agent at a time." Identify
   what is wrong with this claim. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
