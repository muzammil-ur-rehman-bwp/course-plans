# Week 14 Summary — Current Open Problems Survey

**Key takeaways:**
- Scaling expressive-DL reasoning and justification-finding to realistic ontologies without
  losing explainability remains open; modularity helps but is not guaranteed complete.
- Integrating probabilistic and paraconsistent/many-valued semantics into one coherent formalism
  is unresolved — no consensus on what "a probability of a contradictory evidence state" means.
- Guaranteeing neuro-symbolic systems exactly respect symbolic constraints (not just approximately
  on the training distribution) is open; constrained-output projection guarantees exactness at
  the cost of potentially large accuracy loss.

**You should now be able to:** state a specific open problem's precise technical obstacle; name a
partial approach and its limitation; connect an open problem to your capstone's scope.
