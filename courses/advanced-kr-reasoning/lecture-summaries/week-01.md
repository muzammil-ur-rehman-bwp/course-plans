# Week 1 Summary — Postgraduate Overview

**Key takeaways:**
- This course assumes the full graduate KR&R course (modal/temporal logic, ALC tableau, ASP, AGM,
  Dung AF, MLNs, RDF/TransE, epistemic logic, ontology engineering, symbolic explainability) and
  builds past it via four pillars: expressive logics, structured/quantitative non-classical
  reasoning, neuro-symbolic integration/formal verification, and multi-agent/explanatory topics.
- The capstone is a research proposal (problem statement, 5+ paper survey, proposed approach,
  feasibility argument/preliminary results), introduced early so a research direction can take
  shape across the semester.

**You should now be able to:** restate each assumed graduate-course result in one sentence; map
an advanced-KR research question onto this course's week schedule; name a sibling course a given
question is explicitly not about.

**Next week:** Higher-order logic and type theory — why FOL cannot quantify over predicates, and
the simply-typed lambda calculus as a response.
