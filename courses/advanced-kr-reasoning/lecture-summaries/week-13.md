# Week 13 Summary — Research Methods for Advanced KR Research

**Key takeaways:**
- Read a frontier paper abstract → results/examples → formal definitions/proofs → related work →
  full read, to prioritize whether the argument actually establishes the claim.
- A structured critique checks: is the claimed property established or only asserted; are
  examples representative; how does the work relate to this course's formalisms; is the semantics
  fully specified and are complexity claims proved.
- A problem statement names a precise, falsifiable gap (the precision test); a related-work
  survey must relate 5+ papers to each other and motivate the stated gap, not list isolated
  summaries.

**You should now be able to:** critique a frontier KR paper excerpt structurally; apply the
precision test to a research problem statement; annotate related-work papers accurately.
