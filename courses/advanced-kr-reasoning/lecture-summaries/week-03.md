# Week 3 Summary — Many-Valued and Paraconsistent Logics

**Key takeaways:**
- Kleene's K3 and Łukasiewicz's Ł3 agree on ∧,∨,¬ but disagree on implication: K3 gives
  `U → U = U` (determinacy reading), Ł3 gives `U → U = T` (degree-preservation reading).
- Paraconsistent logics (e.g., Belnap–Dunn FDE, with values N/T/F/B) tolerate `φ ∧ ¬φ` (value B)
  without explosion — an unrelated query is not thereby trivially derivable.
- This supports reasoning over genuinely disagreeing information sources without the whole system
  collapsing.

**You should now be able to:** compute K3 and Ł3 truth tables and identify where they diverge;
evaluate an FDE formula and demonstrate non-explosion on a contradictory toy KB.
