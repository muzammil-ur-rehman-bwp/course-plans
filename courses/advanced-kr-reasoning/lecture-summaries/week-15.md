# Week 15 Summary — Research Proposal Work Session

**Key takeaways:**
- The precision test, the survey-synthesis standard, and the feasibility argument's named-risk
  requirement are re-applied one final time before the Week 16 defense.
- The most common proposal weaknesses at this stage are: a problem statement drifting back toward
  a topic; a survey that summarizes accurately but never relates its papers; a feasibility
  argument that lists only reasons the approach will work.
- Structured, committee-style peer feedback across two rotations surfaces exactly the kind of
  pointed questions the real Week 16 committee will ask, while there is still time to respond.

**You should now be able to:** give and receive specific, committee-style proposal feedback;
produce a concrete written revision plan committing to specific responses.
