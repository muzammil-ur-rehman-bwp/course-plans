# Week 13 Lecture Plan — Advanced Knowledge Representation and Reasoning (Post Graduate)
## Topic: Research Methods for Advanced KR Research

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour research seminar/lab

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Critique a frontier KR paper's claimed formal results against its actual argument. (*Analyze,
   Evaluate*)
2. Apply the problem-statement precision test to a candidate research question. (*Apply,
   Evaluate*)
3. Annotate 5+ related-work papers accurately for the capstone survey. (*Analyze*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:25 | Reading a frontier paper | Abstract → results → formal definitions → related work → full read |
| 0:25–0:55 | Structured critique | Claim, evidence, baselines/ablations, reproducibility |
| 0:55–1:20 | Problem-statement precision test | Topic vs. falsifiable question, worked examples |
| 1:20–1:45 | Related-work survey standards | Accurate representation, synthesis, not isolated summaries |
| 1:45–2:00 | Capstone work time | Peer critique of draft problem statements |

### Materials/Equipment
- Slides: "Reading and Critiquing Frontier KR Research"
- A short paper excerpt for the structured-critique in-class exercise

### Formative Check (in-class)
Apply the precision test to a peer's draft problem statement: can you state, from the statement
alone, what evidence would resolve it? If not, name what is missing.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 13: structured paper critique exercise + annotate 5+ candidate related-work papers (see
`lab-manuals/lab-13.md`). **Paper Critique & Presentation assignment assigned** this week.
