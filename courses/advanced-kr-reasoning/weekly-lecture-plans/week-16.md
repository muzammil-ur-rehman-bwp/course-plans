# Week 16 Lecture Plan — Advanced Knowledge Representation and Reasoning (Post Graduate)
## Topic: Capstone Research Proposal Presentations; Course Review

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture (presentations) + 3 hour research seminar/lab (presentations
continued + course review)

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Present and defend an original research proposal under committee-style questioning.
   (*Evaluate, Create*)
2. Recall the full course map and connect it back to the graduate-course foundations. (*Remember,
   Understand*)
3. Situate the course's frontier topics within the open-problems landscape surveyed in Week 14.
   (*Evaluate*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–1:30 | Capstone defenses, Group A | 12–15 min talk + committee Q&A per student |
| 1:30–2:00 | Capstone defenses, Group B (begin) | Continued in the seminar session |
| 2:00–2:40 | Capstone defenses, Group B (continued) | Remaining defenses |
| 2:40–3:00 | Course review | Course-map recap; connection to graduate foundations and open problems |

### Materials/Equipment
- Presentation schedule and committee-question rubric
- `presentations/capstone-presentation-template.md`

### Formative Check (in-class)
Committee members (peers + instructor) pose at least one specific question per defense targeting
the problem statement's precision or the survey's accuracy.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
No lab this week. **Deliverable:** final written research proposal submission + oral defense
(40% Research Proposal Capstone, graded per `assignments/capstone-rubric.md`).
