# Week 15 Lecture Plan — Advanced Knowledge Representation and Reasoning (Post Graduate)
## Topic: Research Proposal Work Session

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture (workshop format) + 3 hour research seminar/lab

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Finalize all four required capstone-proposal components. (*Apply, Create*)
2. Give and receive structured, thesis-committee-style peer feedback. (*Evaluate*)
3. Produce a written revision plan responding to workshop feedback. (*Create*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Workshop format briefing | Committee-style roles; feedback ground rules |
| 0:15–1:10 | Round 1 proposal workshop | Small-group committee review of draft proposals |
| 1:10–1:25 | Break / revision notes | Presenters note specific feedback received |
| 1:25–2:00 | Round 2 proposal workshop | Second small-group rotation, different reviewers |

### Materials/Equipment
- Draft proposals (submitted before session)
- Structured peer-feedback form (problem statement / survey / approach / feasibility)

### Formative Check (in-class)
Each reviewer states, for one proposal reviewed, whether the problem statement passes the
precision test and names the single most likely risk the feasibility argument should address.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 15: incorporate workshop feedback into a written revision plan (see `lab-manuals/lab-15.md`).
**Deliverable:** draft research proposal due this week.
