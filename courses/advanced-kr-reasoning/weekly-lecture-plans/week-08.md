# Week 8 Lecture Plan — Advanced Knowledge Representation and Reasoning (Post Graduate)
## Topic: Neuro-Symbolic Integration II; Midterm Review

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour research seminar/lab

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Explain soft unification in neural theorem proving and contrast it with exact unification.
   (*Understand, Analyze*)
2. Describe how embedding-based link prediction and explicit logical constraints combine.
   (*Analyze*)
3. Review and synthesize Weeks 1–8 for the midterm. (*Remember, Apply, Analyze*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:25 | Neural theorem proving | Soft, embedding-based unification; what is gained/lost |
| 0:25–0:55 | Embeddings + constraints | TransE candidates filtered/vetoed by hard/soft rules |
| 0:55–1:10 | Honest assessment | What neuro-symbolic integration does and does not solve |
| 1:10–2:00 | Midterm review | Practice problems spanning Weeks 1–8 |

### Materials/Equipment
- Slides: "Neural Theorem Proving & Embedding-Constraint Hybrids"
- Midterm practice-problem set

### Formative Check (in-class)
State, in 2–3 sentences, what is gained and what is lost when unification is replaced by a soft,
embedding-similarity score in a neural theorem prover.

Lab 8: filter TransE candidate facts against hand-written hard constraints (see
