# Lab Assignment 14 — Open-Problem Connection Essay (Graded)

**Weight:** part of the weekly Lab Work component (10% of course grade, averaged across all labs).

## Deliverable
Submit your updated capstone-notes document containing complete, substantive responses to Tasks
A–D from `lab-manuals/lab-14.md`.

## Grading Rubric
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Task A correct (obstacle restated precisely, genuinely in the student's own words) | 3 |
| Task B correct (a specific partial approach named, with a specific limitation) | 3 |
| Task C complete (a specific, reasoned scope assessment, not a generic connection) | 3 |
| Task D complete (a genuine revision or a specific justification for no revision) | 1 |
| **Total** | **10** |

## Notes
- This lab is graded on the precision and honesty of the scope assessment, not on whether the
  student's capstone direction turns out, in the end, to need revision.
- A Task A restatement judged too close to the lecture content's own wording will be asked to be
  revised and resubmitted before full credit is given.
