# Lab Assignment 15 — Proposal Workshop and Revision Plan (Graded)

**Weight:** part of the weekly Lab Work component (10% of course grade, averaged across all labs).

## Deliverable
Submit your written revision plan and reviewer notes per Tasks A–D in `lab-manuals/lab-15.md`.

## Grading Rubric
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Task A/C correct (specific, pointed reviewer questions posed in both rotations, documented) | 3 |
| Task B/C complete (specific notes taken on feedback received in both rotations) | 2 |
| Task D correct (a concrete, specific revision plan with a commitment or defensible reason for each item) | 5 |
| **Total** | **10** |

## Notes
- This is the last graded lab before the Week 16 capstone defense; it is graded on engagement
  quality and specificity, not on how "finished" the proposal already is at this stage.
- A revision plan that only restates feedback without committing to a specific response does not
  meet the Task D standard.
