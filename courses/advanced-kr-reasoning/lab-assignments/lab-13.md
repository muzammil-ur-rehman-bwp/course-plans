# Lab Assignment 13 — Structured Paper Critique and Related-Work Annotation (Graded)

**Weight:** part of the weekly Lab Work component (10% of course grade, averaged across all labs).

## Deliverable
Submit your written capstone-notes document containing complete, substantive responses to Tasks
A–D from `lab-manuals/lab-13.md`.

## Grading Rubric
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Task A correct (structured critique complete, claim stated precisely, specific reproducibility concern) | 3 |
| Task B complete (a genuine candidate problem statement, not a placeholder) | 1 |
| Task C correct (revision history shown; final statement passes the precision test) | 3 |
| Task D complete (5+ papers, each annotated with its actual claim and finding) | 3 |
| **Total** | **10** |

## Notes
- This lab is graded on the quality and specificity of critique and annotation, not on whether
  the chosen paper or problem direction turns out to be the student's final capstone topic.
- An annotation that only paraphrases a paper's abstract, without stating its actual finding, does
  not meet the Task D standard regardless of how well-written it is.
