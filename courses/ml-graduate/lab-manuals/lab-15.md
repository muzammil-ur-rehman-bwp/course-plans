# Lab Manual 15 — Structured Capstone Work Session

**Duration:** 3 hours | **Prerequisite:** Week 15 lecture; an approved capstone proposal

## Objectives
Make concrete, instructor-checked progress on the capstone's reproduced/extended experiment or
derivation, ahead of the Week 16 presentation.

## Setup
Open your capstone project's working notebook/repository.

## Procedure
1. **Task A — Literature review check-in:** present your 3–5 selected papers' core claims (one
   sentence each) to the instructor or a peer reviewer for quick feedback.
2. **Task B — Experiment/derivation status:** demonstrate the current state of your reproduced or
   extended experiment or derivation; identify what remains to be done.
3. **Task C — Reproducibility self-check:** apply Week 15's reproducibility checklist (seeds,
   trial counts, stated assumptions, toy-vs-claim gap) to your own in-progress work.
4. **Task D — Draft outline:** produce a one-page draft outline of your written paper and
   presentation, matching the structure in `assignments/capstone-rubric.md`.
5. **Task E — Instructor feedback:** record, in a short markdown cell, the specific feedback
   received and your plan to address it before Week 16.

## Expected Output
A working document/notebook reflecting Tasks A–E; this lab is checked off by instructor
sign-in rather than a single submitted notebook file.

## Submission
Instructor check-in sign-off by the end of the lab session; no separate file submission beyond
your capstone working materials.
