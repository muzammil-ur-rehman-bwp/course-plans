# Week 15 Summary — Research Methods and Project Work Session

**Key takeaways:**
- Reading a theoretical ML paper critically means restating its exact claim, checking its
  assumptions, checking whether its bound is non-vacuous at the regime it's applied to, and
  checking whether any empirical evidence actually tests that claimed regime.
- Reproducibility for theory-adjacent empirical work requires reporting exact assumptions, seeds,
  trial counts, and the gap between a toy illustration and the paper's full claim.
- The Paper Critique & Presentation assignment and structured capstone work time both apply this
  framework directly to real, chosen papers.

**You should now be able to:** critically apply a five-question framework to any theoretical ML
paper, including this course's own stated bounds.
