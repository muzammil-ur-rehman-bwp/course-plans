# Research Capstone — Grading Rubric

**Due:** Week 16 | **Weight:** 25% of course grade total (includes proposal, literature review, experiment/derivation, written paper, presentation)

| Component | Weight (of 25%) | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Proposal | 2% | Clear subtopic connected to course content, feasible scope, sound evaluation plan |
| Literature review | 5% | 3–5 relevant papers accurately summarized *and* compared to one another (not just listed) |
| Reproduced/extended experiment or derivation | 8% | Technically correct; appropriately scoped; validated against a known special case or reference where possible |
| Written paper | 5% | Conference-style structure (problem, related work, method, results, discussion, limitations); honest reporting of what did and did not work |
| Presentation | 5% | Clear 8–10 min conference-style talk; demonstrates understanding under Q&A |

## Grading Notes
- A project that honestly reports a derivation or experiment that did not confirm the expected
  result, with sound analysis of why, can score as well as a project with a clean confirming
  result but weak analysis — understanding and honest reporting matter as much as the outcome.
- Pairs must clearly attribute each member's contribution in the written paper; grading can differ
  between partners if contributions were significantly uneven.
- Reproducing a published result or derivation is expected and encouraged; presenting someone
  else's reported results as one's own original experimental findings is an academic integrity
  violation (see `course-plan.md` §10).
