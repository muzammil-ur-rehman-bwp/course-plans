# Quiz 6 — Causal Inference and Model Selection Theory (Weeks 13–14)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/derivation questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Define confounding and explain how it produces Simpson's paradox. (2 pts)
2. What is the difference between $P(Y\mid X=x)$ and $P(Y\mid\mathrm{do}(X=x))$? (2 pts)
3. Write the bias-variance decomposition for squared error, naming all three terms. (2 pts)
4. State the AIC and BIC formulas and name one way their complexity penalties differ. (2 pts)
5. Why is $k$-fold cross-validation considered an (approximately unbiased) estimator of
   generalization risk? (2 pts)
