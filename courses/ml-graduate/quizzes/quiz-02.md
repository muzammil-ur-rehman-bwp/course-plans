# Quiz 2 — VC Dimension and Rademacher Complexity (Weeks 3–4)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/derivation questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Define shattering and VC dimension precisely. (2 pts)
2. State the VC dimension of linear threshold classifiers in $\mathbb{R}^d$ and of intervals on
   the real line. (2 pts)
3. What does the Sauer–Shelah lemma say, and why is it necessary for the VC bound to extend to
   infinite hypothesis classes? (2 pts)
4. Define empirical Rademacher complexity in one equation. (2 pts)
5. Name the lemma that links Rademacher complexity back to VC dimension/the growth function, and
   state in one sentence what it shows. (2 pts)
