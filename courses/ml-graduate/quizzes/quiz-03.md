# Quiz 3 — Convex Optimization and RKHS Theory (Weeks 6–7)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/derivation questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the gradient-descent convergence rate for a convex, $L$-smooth objective, and for a
   $\mu$-strongly-convex, $L$-smooth objective. (2 pts)
2. What are the four KKT conditions? (2 pts)
3. What does complementary slackness imply about which training points become support vectors in
   an SVM? (2 pts)
4. State the reproducing property of an RKHS, and what it lets you treat as equivalent. (2 pts)
5. State the representer theorem and explain, in one sentence, why it matters computationally. (2 pts)
