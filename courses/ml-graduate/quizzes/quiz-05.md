# Quiz 5 — Structured Prediction and Dimensionality Reduction (Weeks 11–12)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/derivation questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the linear-chain CRF model equation and identify its partition function. (2 pts)
2. In one sentence, state the key difference between a discriminative and a generative
   structured-prediction model, using CRF vs. HMM as the example. (2 pts)
3. State PCA's variance-maximization objective and what the Lagrangian argument shows about its
   optimal solution. (2 pts)
4. What does the Courant–Fischer theorem establish about the top-$k$ eigenvectors? (2 pts)
5. Name one nonlinear dimensionality-reduction method and state, in one sentence, what structure
   it preserves that linear PCA does not. (2 pts)
