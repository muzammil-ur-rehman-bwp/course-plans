# Quiz 4 — Bayesian Machine Learning (Weeks 9–10)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/derivation questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Write the Bayesian linear regression posterior's mean and covariance in closed form. (2 pts)
2. Explain, in one sentence, why the MAP estimate under a Gaussian prior equals the posterior
   mean. (2 pts)
3. State the value of $\lambda$ (in terms of $\sigma^2,\tau^2$) for which the MAP estimate equals
   the ridge-regression solution. (2 pts)
4. Define a Gaussian Process via its defining property over finite sets of function values. (2 pts)
5. Write the GP regression predictive mean and covariance equations. (2 pts)
