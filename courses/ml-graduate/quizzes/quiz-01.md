# Quiz 1 — PAC Learning (Week 2)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/derivation questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the PAC-learnability definition in your own words, naming the roles of $\epsilon$ and
   $\delta$. (2 pts)
2. Write the finite-hypothesis-class sample-complexity bound $m_H(\epsilon,\delta)$ and name the
   two tools (inequalities/arguments) combined to derive it. (2 pts)
3. For $|H|=200$, $\epsilon=0.1$, $\delta=0.1$, compute $m_H(\epsilon,\delta)$. (2 pts)
4. Why does $m_H(\epsilon,\delta)$ depend only logarithmically on $|H|$, not linearly? (2 pts)
5. In the agnostic (non-realizable) setting, what changes about the bound's form, and why? (2 pts)
