3. **Your first program** — `#include`, `main`, `std::cout`/`std::cin` (code snippet slide)

9. **Common pitfalls** — uninitialized variables, off-by-one, infinite loops (checklist slide)
10. **Module 1 recap** — key skills checklist
11. **Looking ahead** — "Next: functions — decomposing programs" teaser slide
