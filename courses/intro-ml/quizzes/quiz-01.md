# Quiz 1 — Intro to ML & Linear Regression (Week 2)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Name the three major learning paradigms covered in Week 1 and give one example application of
   each. (2 pts)
2. Why must a model's test set remain untouched until final evaluation? (2 pts)
3. Write the Mean Squared Error (MSE) cost function for linear regression, in terms of `y_pred`
   and `y_true`. (2 pts)
4. State the normal equation solution for linear regression's optimal weights `theta`. (2 pts)
5. Name one assumption of linear regression and describe, in one sentence, how you would check
   whether it holds. (2 pts)
