# Week 16 Summary — Capstone Presentations & Course Review

**Key takeaways:**
- The course moved from linear models through regularization, classification metrics,
  instance-based and probabilistic models, trees and ensembles, kernel methods, rigorous
  evaluation and feature engineering, unsupervised learning, recommenders, and finally systems
  practice and fairness.
- Three throughlines recur across nearly every week: avoiding data leakage, managing the
  bias-variance tradeoff via hyperparameters, and matching evaluation metrics to the problem's
  actual costs.
- Neural networks are intentionally out of scope here; the dedicated *Introduction to Artificial
  Neural Networks* course (and a further Deep Learning course) picks up from here.

**You should now be able to:** select, justify, tune, and rigorously evaluate an appropriate
classical ML model for a new problem, end to end, and discuss its fairness and deployment
considerations.

**Course complete.** Final Exam is in Week 17, comprehensive with emphasis on Weeks 9–16.
