# Capstone Project — Proposal Guidelines

**Due:** Week 10 | **Weight:** 10% of the Capstone grade (20% total course weight)

## What to Submit
A 1-page proposal (`capstone_proposal.pdf` or `.md`) covering:
1. **Problem statement**: what are you trying to predict, classify, cluster, or recommend?
2. **Dataset**: source, size, and whether it's already available or needs light cleaning (must
   be finalized/accessible by the proposal deadline — no "to be determined" datasets).
3. **Planned algorithm families**: at least three distinct algorithm families from this course
   (e.g., a linear/regularized model, a tree-based or ensemble model, and an SVM or k-NN model)
   that you will train and compare.
4. **Evaluation plan**: which metric(s) you will use and why they fit the problem, and how you
   will validate model selection (e.g., k-fold cross-validation).
5. **Team**: individual or pair, with each member's planned contribution if a pair.

## Approval
The instructor will respond within one week with approval or requested revisions. Projects must
use techniques covered in this course; novel/advanced techniques beyond the syllabus require
instructor pre-approval and are not required for full credit.

## Example Topics (for inspiration, not a closed list)
Predicting housing prices with regression and regularization compared against a Random Forest;
a customer-churn classifier comparing logistic regression, Random Forest, and SVM; a customer
segmentation project combining clustering and PCA; a small movie/product recommender combined
with a classifier predicting whether a user will engage with a recommendation.
