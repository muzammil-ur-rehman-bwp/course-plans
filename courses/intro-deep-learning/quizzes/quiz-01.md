# Quiz 1 — Deep Networks in Practice (Week 2)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. What problem does Xavier/Glorot initialization aim to solve, and how does He initialization
   differ from it? (2 pts)
2. What is the difference between batch normalization's behavior under `model.train()` vs.
   `model.eval()`? (2 pts)
3. Name two reasons dropout helps a network generalize better, as discussed in lecture (the
   regularization view and the implicit ensembling view). (2 pts)
4. What does `torch.nn.utils.clip_grad_norm_` do, and when would you use it? (2 pts)
5. Name one architectural mitigation for vanishing gradients that will be covered in a later week
   (previewed in Week 2). (2 pts)
