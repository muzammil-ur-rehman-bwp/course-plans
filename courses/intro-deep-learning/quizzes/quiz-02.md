# Quiz 2 — CNN Architectures (Week 4)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Write the convolution output-size formula and compute the output size for a 28×28 input with a
   5×5 kernel, stride 2, padding 1. (2 pts)
2. What is the "degradation problem" in very deep plain networks? (2 pts)
3. How does a residual connection change what a block of layers needs to learn, compared to a
   plain stacked block? (2 pts)
4. What architectural idea does VGG standardize on, compared to AlexNet's more varied kernel
   sizes? (2 pts)
5. Why does a convolutional layer's parameter count not grow with image size, unlike a
   fully-connected layer on a flattened image? (2 pts)

before the quiz).**
