# Quiz 4 — Attention (Week 8)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. What is the information bottleneck problem in basic seq2seq models? (2 pts)
2. Name the three steps of computing an attention-based context vector, in order. (2 pts)
3. Along which dimension must softmax be applied when computing attention weights over encoder
   states, and why? (2 pts)
4. What is the difference between dot-product attention and additive (Bahdanau) attention? (2 pts)
5. Why is the context vector recomputed at every decoding step, rather than computed once? (2 pts)
