# Quiz 6 — Optimization and Regularization Revisited (Week 13)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Under plain SGD, why are weight decay and L2 regularization equivalent? (2 pts)
2. Why does this equivalence break down under Adam, and what does AdamW do differently? (2 pts)
3. What does label smoothing change about the training targets, and why? (2 pts)
4. In mixed-precision training, why is a float32 "master" copy of the weights kept alongside
   float16/bfloat16 compute? (2 pts)
5. What is the linear LR scaling rule for large-batch training, and why does warmup matter more as
   batch size grows? (2 pts)
