# Quiz 5 — Autoencoders and VAEs (Week 11)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. What loss does a plain autoencoder minimize, and what labels does it require? (2 pts)
2. Why is a plain autoencoder's latent space not generally suitable for sampling new, realistic
   data? (2 pts)
3. Write the reparameterization trick's formula for `z`, and state which part of it carries no
   gradient. (2 pts)
4. Name the two terms in the ELBO loss and what each one encourages. (2 pts)
5. What is the connection between a linear autoencoder and PCA? (2 pts)
