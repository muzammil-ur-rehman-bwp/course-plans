# Week 15 Summary — Ethics and Current Trends

**Key takeaways:**
- Deep models can amplify, not just reflect, disparities present in skewed training data or proxy
  features, which is a distinct concern from simple accuracy.
- Large-scale model training has a real, growing compute and environmental cost, estimable from
  parameter count and training steps.
- Foundation models and self-supervised pretraining are best understood as extensions of transfer
  learning (Week 5) and the Transformer (Week 9), not as unexplained new magic.
- Evaluating a deep learning system responsibly means weighing its benefits against its bias and
  resource costs, not just its headline accuracy.

**You should now be able to:** analyze a case study for likely sources of bias amplification;
estimate relative training compute cost between two model configurations; explain foundation
models and self-supervised pretraining in grounded, non-hype terms.
