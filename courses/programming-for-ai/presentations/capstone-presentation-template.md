# Capstone Presentation Template (Student-Facing)

**Target length:** 5–7 minutes + Q&A | **Given to students in Week 10 alongside the proposal
guidelines, used for the Week 16 presentation.**

## Required Slides
1. **Title** — project name, your name(s).
2. **Problem statement** — what are you solving, and why does it matter?
3. **Data** — source, size, key characteristics (1–2 sentences + a sample or summary stat).
4. **Approach** — which course technique(s) you applied and why you chose them.
5. **Results** — your evaluation metric(s) and what they show; include at least one chart/table.
6. **Demo** (if applicable) — a live or recorded demonstration of the system working.
7. **Limitations & next steps** — one honest limitation and what you'd try with more time/data.
8. **Questions** — invite audience questions.

## Presentation Tips
- Practice the timing — 5–7 minutes goes faster than it sounds; cut detail, not clarity.
- Lead with the problem, not the code — the audience needs to care about the "why" before the
  "how."
- Show one concrete number or result early; abstract descriptions without evidence are weak.
- Be honest about what didn't work — the rubric (`assignments/capstone-rubric.md`) explicitly
  rewards sound analysis over simply claiming success.
