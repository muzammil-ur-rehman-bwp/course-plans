# Capstone Project — Proposal Guidelines

**Due:** Week 10 | **Weight:** 10% of the Capstone grade (20% total course weight)

## What to Submit
A 1-page proposal (`capstone_proposal.pdf` or `.md`) covering:
1. **Problem statement**: what are you trying to solve/predict/classify/cluster?
2. **Dataset**: source, size, and whether it's already available or needs to be collected
   (must be finalized/accessible by the proposal deadline — no "to be determined" datasets).
3. **Planned technique(s)**: at least one classical AI or ML technique from the course (search,
   CSP, regression, classification, clustering, or neural networks).
4. **Evaluation plan**: how will you measure success (metric(s), and why they fit the problem)?
5. **Team**: individual or pair, with each member's planned contribution if a pair.

## Approval
The instructor will respond within one week with approval or requested revisions. Projects must
use techniques covered in this course; novel/advanced techniques beyond the syllabus require
instructor pre-approval and are not required for full credit.

## Example Topics (for inspiration, not a closed list)
Spam/email classifier, simple game-playing agent via search, handwritten digit recognizer,
customer segmentation via clustering, a small recommendation system, a sentiment classifier.
