## 3. AI Ethics and Responsible Use
- **Bias and fairness**: models trained on biased data reproduce and can amplify that bias;
  evaluating performance across subgroups matters, not just an aggregate metric.
- **Data privacy**: training data may contain sensitive information; understand what data a
  model was trained on and how it's handled.
- **Responsible use of AI-generated code/content**: in this course, AI coding assistants may be
  used per the syllabus's academic integrity policy, but submitted work must be understood and
  attributed honestly — copy-pasting unexamined AI output as your own analysis undermines the
  learning goals of the capstone.
