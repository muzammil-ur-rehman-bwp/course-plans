# Week 8 Summary — Probability & Uncertainty; Midterm Review

**Key takeaways:**
- Conditional probability and Bayes' rule (`P(A|B) = P(B|A)P(A)/P(B)`) let us reason about
  uncertainty and "flip" conditional relationships.
- Naive Bayes classifies by assuming conditional independence of features given the class, and
  uses Laplace smoothing to handle unseen words/features.
- Naive Bayes is the first statistical (as opposed to purely symbolic/search) technique in the
  course, previewing the supervised-learning framing used from Week 9 onward.
- Midterm (Week 9) covers all of Weeks 1–8: Python/NumPy/pandas, search, CSP/local search,
  probability/Bayes.

**You should now be able to:** apply Bayes' rule; implement and explain a Naive Bayes
classifier; self-assess readiness for the midterm.
