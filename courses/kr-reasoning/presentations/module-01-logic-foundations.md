# Presentation: Module 1 — Logic Foundations (Weeks 1–4)

**Format:** Slide deck outline for lecture delivery.

1. **Title slide** — Module 1: Logic Foundations
2. **Why a whole semester on KR&R?** — contrast with `Introduction to AI`'s one-week-per-topic
   survey; this course's depth-first map
3. **KR desiderata** — expressiveness, inferential efficiency, naturalness; the semester's
   representation-scheme map table
4. **Propositional syntax/semantics recap** — connectives, models, satisfiability/validity
5. **Normal forms** — CNF/DNF conversion steps, worked example
6. **The resolution rule** — worked derivation, multiple clause pairs
7. **Resolution refutation** — negate-the-query, derive-the-empty-clause, worked trace
8. **SAT and complexity** — NP-completeness, what it implies for scalability
9. **First-order syntax** — terms, predicates, functions, quantifiers, scope
10. **FOL semantics** — models, satisfaction, the `∀∃`/`∃∀` scope trap worked example
11. **Unification** — MGU, the occurs check, worked trace
12. **FOL resolution & Skolemization** — variable renaming, unify-then-resolve, Skolemization
    worked example; soundness/refutation-completeness (statement only)
13. **Module 1 recap** — roadmap into Module 2 (rules, frames, ontologies)

**Speaker notes:** this module is the most notation-heavy in the course; pace slides 5–7 and
11–12 slowly and work every derivation on the board rather than only showing it pre-filled —
students who only watch a finished derivation rarely retain the step order.
