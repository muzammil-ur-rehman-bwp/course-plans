**Weight:** part of the weekly Lab Work component (20% of course grade, averaged across all labs).

## Deliverable
Submit `lab15.ipynb` with working solutions to Tasks A–D from `lab-manuals/lab-15.md`.

## Grading Rubric
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Task A correct (`KnowledgeGraph` methods correct, including `path_query`) | 3 |
| Task B correct (graph has ≥15 triples, ≥3 predicates, a genuine 2+-hop chain) | 2 |

## Notes
Task D is graded for concreteness: a comparison that only restates "graphs vs. frames are
different representations" without identifying a specific capability (e.g., multi-hop traversal
vs. default-overriding inheritance) earns partial credit only.
