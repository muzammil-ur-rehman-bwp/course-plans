# Week 16 Lecture Plan — Knowledge Representation and Reasoning
## Topic: Capstone Presentations; Course Review

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour lab (used for presentations)

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Present a capstone knowledge-based reasoning system integrating at least two course
   formalisms, and defend its design in Q&A. (*Evaluate, Create*)
2. Evaluate peers' capstone projects along the correctness/scalability/explainability axes from
   Week 15. (*Evaluate*)
3. Recall the full course map and articulate how it extends beyond a brief classical-AI survey.
   (*Remember, Understand*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–1:40 | Capstone presentations | 5–7 minutes per team + Q&A (lecture and lab slots combined for presentations) |
| 1:40–2:00 | Course review | Recap of the full course map; how it extends `Introduction to AI`'s brief survey |

### Materials/Equipment
- Presentation schedule and room/AV setup
- `presentations/capstone-presentation-template.md` (distributed to students in Week 11)

### Formative Check (in-class)
Each team completes a short peer-feedback form for at least one other team's presentation,
naming one strength and one question tied to correctness, scalability, or explainability.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
No new lab this week; the lab session is used for capstone presentations. Final capstone
submission (`assignments/capstone-rubric.md`) is due at the start of this week.
