# Quiz 4 — Description Logics & Constraint Satisfaction (Week 8)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. What does the DL constructor `∃R.C` mean, in plain English? (2 pts)
2. Why are description logics decidable while full first-order logic is not? (2 pts)
3. What does AC-3's `revise(domains, xi, xj, constraint)` subroutine remove, and why is doing so
   always safe? (2 pts)
4. Does arc consistency guarantee a CSP solution exists? Justify your answer in one sentence.
   (2 pts)
5. What does the minimum-remaining-values (MRV) heuristic choose, and why does it tend to speed
   up backtracking search? (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
