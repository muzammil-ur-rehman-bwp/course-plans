# Quiz 6 — Temporal Reasoning, Bayesian Networks & Fuzzy Logic (Week 14)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Given intervals `(1, 4)` and `(4, 7)`, which Allen relation holds between them? (1 pt)
2. In variable elimination, what two operations are applied to eliminate a hidden variable?
   (2 pts)
3. Does the order in which hidden variables are eliminated change the final answer of variable
   elimination? Does it change its efficiency? (2 pts)
4. What is a membership function in fuzzy logic, and what range of values can it return? (2 pts)
5. In Markov logic networks, what distinguishes a "soft" weighted formula from a classical, hard
   FOL constraint? (2 pts)
