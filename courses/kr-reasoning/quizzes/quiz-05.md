# Quiz 5 — Non-Monotonic Reasoning & Planning (Week 11)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. What does it mean for a logic to be "monotonic," and why is this the wrong model for
   common-sense reasoning with exceptions? (2 pts)
2. Under the closed-world assumption, what happens to an atomic fact that is not derivable from
   the KB? (2 pts)
3. In default logic, what two conditions must hold for a default's consequent to be concluded?
   (2 pts)
4. What is a "threat" in partial-order planning, and name one way to resolve it. (2 pts)
5. What does grounding a STRIPS action schema produce? (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
