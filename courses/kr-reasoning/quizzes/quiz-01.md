# Quiz 1 — KR Desiderata & Propositional Logic (Week 2)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the three desiderata for a good knowledge representation. (2 pts)
2. What does `KB ⊨ α` mean? (2 pts)
3. Convert `P → Q` to its CNF clause form (implication elimination). (2 pts)
4. Given clauses `{P, Q}` and `{~P, R}`, what is their resolvent? (2 pts)
5. Why is SAT's NP-completeness relevant to the scalability of resolution-based reasoning?
   (2 pts)
