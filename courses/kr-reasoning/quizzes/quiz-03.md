# Quiz 3 — Rule-Based Systems & Semantic Networks/Frames (Week 6)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Name the three parts of a production system. (2 pts)
2. What is the key difference between forward chaining and backward chaining? (2 pts)
3. Using the Tweety/penguin example, explain in 1–2 sentences why strict property inheritance
   gives the wrong answer. (2 pts)
4. In a frame system, in what order does `get_slot` check for a value, from most to least
   specific? (2 pts)
5. Why must a general-purpose rule engine avoid referencing any domain-specific fact or
   predicate name internally? (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
