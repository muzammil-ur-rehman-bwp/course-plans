# Quiz 2 — First-Order Logic & Unification (Week 4)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Translate "every student has taken at least one course" into FOL. (2 pts)
2. Explain, in one sentence, why `∀x∃y Likes(x,y)` is not equivalent to `∃y∀x Likes(x,y)`.
   (2 pts)
3. What is the most general unifier of `Likes(x, mother(x))` and `Likes(john, mother(john))`?
   (2 pts)
4. Why does unifying `x` with `f(x)` fail the occurs check? (2 pts)
5. What must be done to two clauses' variables before they can be safely resolved in FOL
   resolution, and why? (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
