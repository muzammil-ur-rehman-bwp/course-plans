- Capstone presentations demonstrate a knowledge-based reasoning system integrating at least two
  formalisms from the course, evaluated on correctness, scalability, and explainability.

  Intelligence`.

  scale and where they connect to the learning-based pillar of modern AI.

along the correctness/scalability/explainability axes; articulate, concretely, how this course's
