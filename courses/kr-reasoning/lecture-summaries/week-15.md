# Week 15 Summary — Current Trends in Knowledge Representation

**Key takeaways:**
- Knowledge graphs scale the triple-store/semantic-network idea to production, supporting
  multi-hop pattern queries over large fact collections.
- Neuro-symbolic AI combines symbolic KR with learned components in either direction — learning
  proposing facts for a reasoner, or symbolic constraints guiding a learned model — at a survey
  level connecting this course to the ML-centric pillar of AI.
- KR systems are evaluated along three axes — correctness, scalability, explainability — that
  recur throughout this course and apply to judging any KR system, including the capstone.

**You should now be able to:** implement a knowledge-graph triple store with multi-hop pattern
queries; describe neuro-symbolic AI's two directions of combination at a survey level; evaluate a
KR system along the correctness/scalability/explainability axes.
