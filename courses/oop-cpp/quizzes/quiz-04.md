# Quiz 4 — Inheritance I & II (Week 7)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Why must a derived class's constructor arrange for a base-class constructor to run, when the
   base class has no accessible default constructor? (2 pts)
2. What does the `override` keyword do, and what kind of bug does it help catch at compile time?
   (2 pts)
3. In one or two sentences, explain name hiding and how it differs from overriding. (2 pts)
4. Draw or describe a diamond-inheritance shape (two base classes sharing a common ancestor) and
   state what becomes ambiguous about a most-derived object. (2 pts)
5. In what order are a `Dog : public Animal` object's destructors called when the object is
   destroyed? (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
