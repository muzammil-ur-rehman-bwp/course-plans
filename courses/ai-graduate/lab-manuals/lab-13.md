# Lab Manual 13 — Structured Paper Critique & Ablation Design

**Duration:** 3 hours | **Prerequisite:** Week 13 lecture

## Objectives
Apply the structured critique worksheet to a short paper excerpt and design an ablation study
for a hypothetical AI system.

## Setup
1. No special software setup; this lab is primarily a written/discussion exercise, optionally
   recorded in a notebook or document.
2. Obtain the instructor-provided short paper excerpt and the structured critique worksheet.

## Procedure
1. **Task A — Claim and evidence:** read the provided excerpt; state its central claim in one
   or two sentences, then list the specific evidence (figures, numbers, tables) offered for it.
2. **Task B — Baseline and reproducibility check:** identify the baseline(s) compared against
   and assess whether they appear current/fair; list at least one reproducibility concern
   (missing hyperparameters, seeds, compute disclosure, or code/data availability) from the
   excerpt.
3. **Task C — Ablation design:** for a hypothetical system combining two components (A and B,
   as specified by the instructor), design an ablation study: specify the conditions
   ("A only," "B only," "A and B," baseline with neither) and what each isolates.
4. **Task D — Mini-challenge:** apply `summarize_runs`/`naive_significance_gap` from the Week 13
   lecture content to a provided small dataset of repeated-run results for two hypothetical
   methods, and state whether the evidence supports a confident claim that one method is better.

## Expected Output
A short written document (or notebook) with four clearly labeled sections (A–D).

## Submission
Submit the completed worksheet/notebook via the course submission system by the end of the lab
session.
