# Lab Manual 15 — Guided Capstone Work Session

**Duration:** 3 hours | **Prerequisite:** Week 15 lecture; an in-progress capstone project

## Objectives
Use structured lab time to advance the research capstone's experiment and writing, and to
complete a peer-feedback exchange on a practice presentation.

## Setup
No new software setup; bring your own capstone project's code/notebook environment and current
draft materials (literature review notes, experiment code, draft paper).

## Procedure
1. **Task A — Experiment progress:** run (or continue running) your capstone experiment; record
   at least one concrete intermediate result (even partial/preliminary) in your working notebook
   or document.
2. **Task B — Literature review check:** verify your literature review explicitly states the
   gap or question your experiment addresses (per the Week 15 lecture content's guidance);
   revise if it does not.
3. **Task C — Practice talk & peer feedback:** give (or receive) a 3–4 minute practice talk;
   complete the structured peer-feedback worksheet (clarity of problem statement, experiment
   soundness, honesty about limitations) for a classmate's talk.
4. **Task D — Work-session note:** write a short (half-page) work-session note: what you
   accomplished, what feedback you received, and what you will do before the Week 15 draft-paper
   deadline.

## Expected Output
A short work-session note (Task D), plus whatever experiment/writing progress was made in Tasks
A–C, submitted alongside (or as part of) the capstone draft-paper submission.

## Submission
Submit the work-session note and completed peer-feedback worksheet via the course submission
system by the end of the lab session.
