# Lab Assignment 15 — Guided Capstone Work Session (Graded)

**Weight:** part of the weekly Lab Work component (15% of course grade, averaged across all labs).
This is the final lab of the semester (no lab in Week 16; Week 16 is capstone presentations).

## Deliverable
Submit the Task D work-session note and the completed peer-feedback worksheet from
`lab-manuals/lab-15.md`, alongside your capstone draft-paper progress.

## Grading Rubric
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Task A/B evidence of genuine experiment/literature-review progress this session | 4 |
| Task C peer-feedback worksheet completed with specific, actionable comments | 3 |
| Task D work-session note is specific and reflects actual session activity | 3 |
| **Total** | **10** |

## Notes
- This lab is graded on genuine engagement and documented progress, not on a "correct answer" —
  a note that honestly describes a stuck point and a concrete plan to address it scores as well
  as one describing smooth progress.
- Partial credit given for partial engagement; no credit for a note/worksheet clearly not
  reflecting real session work.
