# Week 16 Lecture Plan — Artificial Intelligence (Graduate)
## Topic: Capstone Research Presentations; Course Review

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour lab/seminar (both reallocated to presentations)

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Present an original research-style project in a conference-talk format and respond to Q&A.
   (*Create, Evaluate*)
2. Evaluate peers' presentations against the capstone rubric's criteria. (*Evaluate*)
3. Recall and synthesize the course's full map: advanced search → game theory/multi-agent
   systems → automated reasoning → rigorous planning → MDPs/RL/POMDPs → graphical-model
   inference → AI complexity → research methods → current trends. (*Remember, Understand*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Logistics | Presentation order, timing rules reminder |
| 0:10–1:40 | Capstone presentations, round 1 | Student/pair talks (per `presentations/capstone-presentation-template.md`), Q&A each |
| 1:40–1:55 | Break | — |
| (lab block) | Capstone presentations, round 2 | Remaining talks, Q&A each |
| Final 20 min | Course review | Recap of the full course map; discussion of sibling graduate courses ahead |

### Materials/Equipment
- Projector/screen for student presentations
- Capstone rubric for instructor scoring (`assignments/capstone-rubric.md`)
- Course-map summary slide for the closing review

### Formative Check (in-class)
Each student completes a short peer-evaluation form for at least two other presentations,
referencing the same three axes used in Week 15's peer-feedback practice.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
**Capstone final paper + presentation due** (25% of course grade; see `assignments/capstone-
rubric.md`). No new lab assigned this week.
