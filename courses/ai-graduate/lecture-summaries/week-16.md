# Week 16 Summary — Capstone Research Presentations; Course Review

**Key takeaways:**
- Capstone presentations are scored against the published rubric: literature review,
  experimental soundness, honest analysis (including limitations), written paper, presentation.
- The course moved through five phases: graduate foundations; advanced search and game theory;
  automated reasoning and rigorous planning; decision-theoretic AI (MDPs/RL/POMDPs/graphical
  models); and complexity/research methods/capstone.
- Three threads ran throughout: formal correctness arguments, real complexity results used to
  justify design choices (not just named), and research methods as a graded, practiced skill.

**You should now be able to:** present an original research project in conference-talk format;
evaluate a presentation against a structured rubric; situate this course's content relative to
the sibling graduate AI-family courses (ANN, ML, DL, KR&R).

**Course complete.** Final Exam (Week 17) is comprehensive, with emphasis on Weeks 9–14.
