# Week 15 Summary — Research Project Work Session

**Key takeaways:**
- A strong literature review ends with an explicit stated gap or question, not just a list of
  paper summaries.
- The capstone experiment design should specify benchmark/task, baseline, ablation (if
  applicable), and multiple random seeds with reported spread for any stochastic method.
- Structured peer feedback targets three specific axes: clarity of problem statement, soundness
  of experiment, and honesty about limitations — not a vague overall impression.

**You should now be able to:** finalize a literature review with a stated gap; check an
experiment design against Week 13's checklist; give and act on specific, actionable peer
feedback.

**Next week:** Capstone research presentations (conference-talk format) and course review.
