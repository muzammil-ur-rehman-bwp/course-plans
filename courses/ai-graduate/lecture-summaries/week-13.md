# Week 13 Summary — Research Methods in AI

**Key takeaways:**
- Read papers abstract/figures-first, not linearly front-to-back, to triage quickly.
- A structured critique separates claim, evidence, baseline adequacy, ablations, and
  reproducibility — each is a distinct, checkable question, not a single "is this good?" judgment.
- Common reproducibility failures: missing hyperparameters, cherry-picked seeds, undisclosed
  compute, non-public code/data.
- Sound experimental design requires appropriate benchmarks, strong current baselines, ablation
  studies isolating each component's contribution, and attention to statistical significance
  across multiple random seeds — a single run is not evidence.

**You should now be able to:** critique a paper using a structured framework; design an ablation
study; explain why multiple-seed comparisons are necessary before claiming one method is better.
