# Quiz 1 — Advanced Search (Week 2)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. What is the space complexity of IDA*, and how does it compare to plain A*'s worst-case space
   complexity? (2 pts)
2. In IDA*, how is the threshold for the next iteration chosen? (2 pts)
3. Under what condition does bidirectional search give an exponential speedup over a
   unidirectional search, and what practical requirement does it place on the transition model?
   (2 pts)
4. Briefly describe how SMA* behaves when it runs out of memory — what does it discard, and what
   does it do with the discarded node's value? (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "IDA* is always faster than A* because it uses less memory." Identify
   what is wrong with this claim. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
