# Quiz 5 — MDPs, Q-Learning, POMDPs & Probabilistic Inference (Week 11)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Write the Q-learning update rule, and state whether Q-learning is on-policy or off-policy. (2 pts)
2. Why might an agent using a purely greedy (ε = 0) policy from an all-zero initial Q-table never
   learn the optimal policy? (2 pts)
3. What is a "belief state" in a POMDP, and why is it described as a sufficient statistic for the
   agent's action/observation history? (2 pts)
4. What is the key practical difference between rejection sampling and likelihood weighting when
   the evidence being conditioned on is rare? (2 pts)
5. True or false, with one sentence of justification: exact inference in every Bayesian network
   can always be computed in time polynomial in the number of variables. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
