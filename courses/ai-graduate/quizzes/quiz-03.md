# Quiz 3 — CSP, Optimization & Automated Reasoning (Week 6)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. What does it mean for an arc (Xi, Xj) to be "arc consistent," and what does AC-3 do when it
   finds an inconsistent arc? (2 pts)
2. In simulated annealing, what is the Metropolis acceptance criterion, and what happens to the
   probability of accepting a worsening move as the temperature T decreases toward 0? (2 pts)
3. True or false, with one sentence of justification: genetic algorithms have the same
   asymptotic convergence-to-global-optimum guarantee that simulated annealing has under a
   sufficiently slow cooling schedule. (2 pts)
4. In DPLL, what is "unit propagation," and why must it be applied to a fixed point (repeatedly,
   not just once) before branching? (2 pts)
5. What is the key difference between a plain SAT solver and an SMT solver? (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
