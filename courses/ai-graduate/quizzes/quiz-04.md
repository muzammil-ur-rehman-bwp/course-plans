# Quiz 4 — Planning Complexity & MDP Foundations (Week 8)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. What complexity class is general STRIPS plan existence complete for, and which is believed to
   be a (possibly strict) superset of NP? (2 pts)
2. What does the relaxed planning graph heuristic ignore about actions, and why does this
   simplification make it cheaper to compute? (2 pts)
3. Write the Bellman optimality equation for V*(s). (2 pts)
4. A student runs value iteration with γ = 1.0 on an MDP with no guaranteed-absorbing terminal
   state and finds the values do not converge. Explain why, referencing the contraction-mapping
   argument. (2 pts)
5. What is the key structural idea behind HTN planning that flat STRIPS forward search does not
   exploit? (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
