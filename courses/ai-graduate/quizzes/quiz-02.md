# Quiz 2 — Game Theory & Multi-Agent Systems (Week 4)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the formal definition of a Nash equilibrium for a 2-player normal-form game. (2 pts)
2. True or false, with one sentence of justification: a Nash equilibrium is always the outcome
   that maximizes the sum of both players' payoffs. (2 pts)
3. In the alpha-beta pruning algorithm, what condition at a MAX node triggers a cutoff, and why
   does this cutoff never change the value ultimately returned to the root? (2 pts)
4. In a second-price (Vickrey) auction, what price does the winning bidder pay, and why does this
   make truthful bidding a dominant strategy? (2 pts)
5. Classify the following scenario as cooperative/competitive and zero-sum/general-sum: two
   warehouse robots must jointly minimize total delivery time by choosing which of two routes
   each will take. (2 pts)

before the quiz).**
