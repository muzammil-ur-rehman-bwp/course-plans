# Quiz 6 — AI Complexity, Research Methods & Current Trends (Week 14)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. What does it mean for SAT to be "NP-complete" (state both parts: membership and hardness)? (2 pts)
2. A paper reports a single run showing Method A outperforms Method B. Explain, in 1–2
   sentences, why this alone is not sufficient evidence that Method A is genuinely better. (2 pts)
3. Name two of the four common reproducibility concerns discussed in Week 13 (e.g., missing
   hyperparameters, cherry-picked seeds, undisclosed compute, non-public code/data). (2 pts)
4. Why is multi-agent reinforcement learning harder than single-agent reinforcement learning,
   from the perspective of any one learning agent? (2 pts)
5. Give one concrete example of "reward hacking" / reward misspecification in a toy RL scenario,
   and briefly state the gap between the stated reward and the designer's true intent. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
