# Capstone Project — Proposal Guidelines

**Due:** Week 10 | **Weight:** 10% of the Capstone grade (20% total course weight)

## What to Submit
A 1-page proposal (`capstone_proposal.pdf` or `.md`) covering:
1. **Behavior statement**: what autonomous behavior will your robot perform (e.g., line
   following, obstacle avoidance, pick-and-place sequence, vision-guided goal-seeking)?
2. **Simulation setup**: which simulator (Gazebo/Webots), robot model, and world/obstacle layout
   you'll use (must be feasible with the course's provided tools — no physical hardware required,
   though optional if available and approved).
3. **Planned technique(s)**: at least three course techniques integrated together (e.g.,
   kinematics + PID control + path planning, or vision-based detection + control).
4. **Evaluation plan**: how will you measure success (e.g., success rate reaching the goal,
   path efficiency, time to completion)?
5. **Team**: individual or small group, with each member's planned contribution if a group.

## Approval
The instructor will respond within one week with approval or requested revisions. Projects must
use techniques covered in this course; novel/advanced techniques beyond the syllabus require
instructor pre-approval and are not required for full credit.

## Example Topics (for inspiration, not a closed list)
Line-following robot, obstacle-avoiding mobile robot, simple pick-and-place arm sequence,
vision-guided goal-seeking robot, a small multi-waypoint delivery simulation.
