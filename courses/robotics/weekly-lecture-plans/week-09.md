# Week 9 Lecture Plan — Robotics
## Topic: Midterm Exam + Sensors II — Range Sensing

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour lab (exam occupies part of lecture time)

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Demonstrate recall and application of Weeks 1–8 material under exam conditions. (*Remember, Apply*)
2. Explain how LiDAR/range sensors work and how their data forms an occupancy grid. (*Understand*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–1:00 | **Midterm Exam** | Closed-book/open-notes per instructor policy, covers Weeks 1–8 |
| 1:00–1:10 | Break | — |
| 1:10–1:40 | Range sensors | LiDAR/ultrasonic/IR principles, scan message structure |
| 1:40–2:00 | Occupancy grids | Converting a range scan into a grid representation |

### Materials/Equipment
- Midterm exam paper/online quiz
- ROS 2 (`sensor_msgs/LaserScan`), simulated LiDAR in Gazebo/Webots

### Formative Check (in-class)
Post-exam discussion: given a simulated LiDAR scan, sketch the corresponding occupancy grid by
hand.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 9: visualize a simulated LiDAR scan and build a simple occupancy grid from it.
