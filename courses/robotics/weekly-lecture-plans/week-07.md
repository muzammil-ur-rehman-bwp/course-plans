# Week 7 Lecture Plan — Robotics
## Topic: Robot Dynamics & Actuators

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour lab

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Explain mass, inertia, torque, and force as they relate to robot motion. (*Understand*)
2. Explain how DC motors and servo motors produce controlled motion. (*Understand*)
3. Apply basic torque calculations to a simple arm/wheel scenario. (*Apply*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | Dynamics basics | Mass, inertia, torque, force — definitions and relationships |
| 0:30–1:00 | Actuators | DC motors, servo motors, PWM control signals (conceptual) |
| 1:00–1:10 | Break | — |
| 1:10–1:40 | Torque-speed tradeoff | Gearing, worked example for a wheeled robot |
| 1:40–2:00 | Worked example | Compute required torque for a robot arm to hold a given load |

### Materials/Equipment
- Slides: "Actuators and Dynamics Basics"
- Live-coding environment (calculations only, no simulator needed)

### Formative Check (in-class)
Exercise: given a robot arm's load and link length, compute the required motor torque at the
shoulder joint.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 7: torque/force calculation exercises relating simulated actuator commands to physical motor
behavior.
