# Week 15 Summary — Research Methods and Capstone Work Session

**Key takeaways:**
- A paper critique should extract the precise claim, its assumptions, the strength of its
  evidence, and its limitations — distinguishing existence results from practical/empirical ones.
- Reproducibility requires reporting multiple seeds with spread, documenting hyperparameters, and
  comparing fairly-tuned baselines, since seed variance and undocumented settings are common,
  well-documented sources of irreproducibility.
- Capstone work time should produce a sharpened literature-review gap statement and a concretely
  scoped experiment/derivation plan, ready for the Week 16 presentation.

**You should now be able to:** apply a structured critique checklist to any theoretical ML paper
and assess your own planned capstone experiment's reproducibility.
