# Week 16 Summary — Capstone Research Presentations; Course Review

**Key takeaways:**
- Capstone presentations demonstrate the full research cycle this course built toward: literature
  review, a reproduced or extended experiment/derivation, honest reporting, and a conference-style
  talk.
- The course's six pillars (approximation, autodiff, initialization/normalization,
  optimization-landscape, generalization, and modern research directions) together form a
  collection of partial, rigorous accounts — no single pillar fully explains why deep learning
  works, and that is the field's honest current state.

  current theoretical ML venues, are the natural next steps.

identify at least one specific claim or intuition this course taught you to hold more carefully.
