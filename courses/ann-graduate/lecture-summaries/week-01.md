# Week 1 Summary — Graduate ANN Theory Overview

**Key takeaways:**
- This course assumes, and does not re-teach, the perceptron, the MLP, hand-derived
  backpropagation, and basic optimizers/regularization from *Introduction to Artificial Neural
  Networks*.
- The course is organized around six theoretical pillars: approximation theory, automatic
  differentiation, initialization/normalization theory, optimization-landscape theory,
  generalization theory, and modern research directions.
- This course is scoped away from architecture depth (owned by the sibling Deep Learning,
  Graduate course) and classical ML algorithms (owned by Machine Learning, Graduate).

**You should now be able to:** state the six theoretical pillars this course covers; identify
whether your own prerequisite fluency has gaps to close before Week 2.
