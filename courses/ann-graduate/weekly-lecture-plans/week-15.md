# Week 15 Lecture Plan — Artificial Neural Network (Graduate)
## Topic: Research Methods and Capstone Work Session

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour lab/seminar

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Apply a structured framework to critique a theoretical machine learning paper's claims and
   methodology. (*Analyze, Evaluate*)
2. Explain what reproducibility requires in machine learning research and why exact numbers often
   fail to reproduce. (*Understand, Analyze*)
3. Advance the capstone literature review and experiment during structured work time. (*Create*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | How to critique a paper | Claim, assumptions, evidence strength, limitations — a repeatable checklist |
| 0:20–0:45 | Guided critique exercise | Students apply the checklist to a short provided paper excerpt, in pairs |
| 0:45–1:10 | Reproducibility | Seed variance, undocumented hyperparameters, hardware/library-version differences; what a reproducibility-conscious report includes (multiple seeds with spread, not one run) |
| 1:10–2:00 | Capstone work session | Structured literature-review and experiment work time; instructor consultations by table |

### Materials/Equipment
- Paper-critique checklist handout
- Short paper excerpt for the guided exercise
- Capstone milestone checklist

### Formative Check (in-class)
Each pair submits their one-paragraph critique of the guided excerpt, explicitly naming the
claim, one assumption it rests on, and one limitation.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 15: Apply the critique framework to the student's own chosen capstone paper(s) and produce a
written reproducibility assessment of the capstone's planned experiment (see
`lab-manuals/lab-15.md`). **Paper Critique & Presentation due. Capstone draft due.**
