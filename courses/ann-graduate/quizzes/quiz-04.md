# Quiz 4 — Generalization Theory II (Week 10)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. In one or two sentences, define empirical Rademacher complexity. (2 pts)
2. Why is Rademacher complexity often described as "data-dependent" in a way VC dimension is not?
   (2 pts)
3. A classifier separates its training data with a larger margin than another classifier from the
   same hypothesis class. What does margin-based theory suggest about their relative
   generalization, all else equal? (2 pts)
4. A network fits entirely random training labels to zero training error. Does this, by itself,
   imply the same network will overfit when trained normally on real labels? Answer yes/no and
   justify in one sentence. (2 pts)
5. What is the one property of a hypothesis class that both the random-label-fitting experiment
   and raw VC dimension are both evidence about? (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
