# Quiz 3 — Optimization Landscape Theory I (Week 6)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Given a Hessian with eigenvalues $\{3, -1, 2\}$, classify the critical point. (2 pts)
2. In one or two sentences, explain why saddle points become overwhelmingly more common than true
   local minima as the number of parameters grows. (2 pts)
3. Why can plain Newton's method be actively attracted to a saddle point, rather than merely slow
   near one (as gradient descent is)? (2 pts)
4. State the asymptotic storage cost, in the number of parameters $p$, of forming the full
   Hessian matrix. (2 pts)
5. What positive-semi-definiteness property does the Gauss-Newton approximation have that the
   exact Hessian does not always have? (2 pts)

before the quiz).**
