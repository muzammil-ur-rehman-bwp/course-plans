# Quiz 1 — Universal Approximation (Week 2)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the Universal Approximation Theorem for single-hidden-layer sigmoidal networks in one or
   two sentences. (2 pts)
2. Name one thing the theorem does **not** guarantee about training such a network with gradient
   descent. (2 pts)
3. In the bump-function proof sketch, what two basic operations on shifted sigmoids combine to
   produce one localized bump? (2 pts)
4. True or false, with one sentence of justification: a function that needs exponentially many
   units in a shallow network can sometimes be represented with only polynomially many units if
   depth is allowed. (2 pts)
5. Why does the theorem give no fixed bound on the number of hidden units required for a given
   target function? (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
