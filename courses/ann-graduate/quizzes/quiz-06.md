# Quiz 6 — The Lottery Ticket Hypothesis and Pruning (Week 13)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the Lottery Ticket Hypothesis in one or two sentences. (2 pts)
2. List, in order, the 5 steps of iterative magnitude pruning. (2 pts)
3. In the winning-ticket procedure, surviving weights are reset to which values before
   retraining? (2 pts)
4. What is the one change made in the random-reinitialization control, relative to the winning
   ticket condition, and why is isolating exactly that one change important? (2 pts)
5. In one sentence, distinguish knowledge distillation from magnitude pruning as a model
   compression technique. (2 pts)

before the quiz).**
