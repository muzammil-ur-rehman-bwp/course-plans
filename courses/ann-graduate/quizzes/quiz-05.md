# Quiz 5 — The Neural Tangent Kernel (Week 12)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Write the formula for the Neural Tangent Kernel $\Theta(x,x';\theta)$ in terms of
   $\nabla_\theta f$. (2 pts)
2. Is the NTK computed from the gradient of the network's output with respect to its
   **parameters** or with respect to its **input**? (2 pts)
3. State, in one sentence, Jacot, Gabriel, and Hongler's central claim about infinite-width
   networks trained with gradient descent. (2 pts)
4. What specific capability do NTK-regime (infinite-width) networks provably lack, that is widely
   believed to matter for realistic finite-width networks' strong generalization? (2 pts)
5. As network width grows in a finite-width experiment, would you expect kernel-regression
   predictions using the empirical NTK to agree with the trained network's predictions more, or
   less, closely? (2 pts)
