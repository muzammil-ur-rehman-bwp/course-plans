# Week 9 Lecture Plan — Introduction to AI
## Topic: Midterm Exam + Classical Planning

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour lab (exam occupies part of lecture time)

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Demonstrate recall and application of Weeks 1–8 material under exam conditions. (*Remember, Apply*)
2. Explain the STRIPS representation (preconditions, add-list, delete-list) for an action. (*Understand*)
3. Apply forward state-space search over STRIPS actions to construct a simple plan. (*Apply*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–1:00 | **Midterm Exam** | Closed-book/open-notes per instructor policy, covers Weeks 1–8 |
| 1:00–1:10 | Break | — |
| 1:10–1:30 | Classical planning intro | Why planning is search with a structured action representation; STRIPS actions |
| 1:30–2:00 | Worked planning example | A small blocks-world domain: initial state, goal, STRIPS actions, forward search to a plan |

### Materials/Equipment
- Midterm exam paper/online quiz
- Slides: STRIPS action schema, blocks-world worked example

### Formative Check (in-class)
Post-exam discussion: given a STRIPS action's preconditions and effects, decide whether it is
applicable in a given state and compute the resulting state.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 9: Implement a tiny STRIPS-style planner (forward state-space search) for a toy blocks-world
domain.
