# Week 8 Lecture Plan — Introduction to AI
## Topic: First-Order Logic; Midterm Review

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour lab

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. State the syntax of first-order logic: constants, predicates, functions, variables, and quantifiers (∀, ∃). (*Remember*)
2. Explain FOL semantics in terms of objects and relations. (*Understand*)
3. Apply FOL to translate English sentences into logical form and vice versa. (*Apply*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Limits of propositional logic | Why we need objects, relations, and quantification |
| 0:15–0:45 | FOL syntax | Constants, predicates, functions, variables, ∀ and ∃, scope of quantifiers |
| 0:45–1:00 | Break | — |
| 1:00–1:30 | FOL semantics | Models with a domain of objects and interpreted relations/functions |
| 1:30–1:50 | English-to-FOL translation | Worked examples ("All students who study pass the exam", "Some cats are black") |
| 1:50–2:00 | Midterm review roadmap | Topics covered (Weeks 1–7), format, practice-problem pointers |

### Materials/Equipment
- Slides: FOL syntax cheat sheet, English-to-FOL worked examples
- Midterm review handout (practice problems covering Weeks 1–7)

### Formative Check (in-class)
Exercise: translate 5 English sentences into FOL in pairs; class discusses tricky quantifier-
scope cases ("Every student has a favorite course" vs. "There is one favorite course every
student shares").

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 8: Translate a set of English statements into FOL (paper exercise); implement a simple
well-formedness checker for FOL sentence strings in Python.
