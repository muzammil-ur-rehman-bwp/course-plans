# Week 16 Lecture Plan — Introduction to AI
## Topic: Capstone Presentations + Course Review

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour lab (both sessions used for presentations)

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Present and defend a capstone project applying a classical AI technique from the course. (*Create, Evaluate*)
2. Evaluate peers' projects by asking substantive questions about technique choice and evaluation. (*Evaluate*)
3. Summarize the course's conceptual map from agents through search, logic, planning, uncertainty, and the ML/NLP/vision/robotics survey. (*Understand, Evaluate*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Logistics | Presentation order, timing rules (5–7 min + Q&A), rubric reminder |
| 0:10–1:30 | Capstone presentations (part 1) | Student/pair presentations with Q&A |
| 1:30–1:40 | Break | — |
| 1:40–2:00 | Capstone presentations (part 2) | Remaining presentations with Q&A (continues into lab session if the class is large) |
| Lab session | Course review | Recap of the full course map; open Q&A for the final exam |

### Materials/Equipment
- Projector/screen for student presentations
- `assignments/capstone-rubric.md` for live grading
- Course-map recap slide (Week 1 → Week 15 pipeline)

### Formative Check (in-class)
Each student/pair in the audience submits one written question or observation for a project
other than their own, used lightly to inform class participation.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Capstone final submission and presentation (20% of course grade). No new lab is assigned this
week.
