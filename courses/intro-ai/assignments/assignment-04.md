## Questions
1. **(Decision Trees, 20 pts)** Given a small toy dataset (6–8 examples, provided by the
   instructor, different from the lecture's "Play Tennis?" example), compute entropy and
   information gain by hand for each candidate attribute, build the first split, and verify with
   your Lab 12 `information_gain` implementation.
2. **(Perceptron, 20 pts)** By hand, trace 3 weight updates of the perceptron learning rule on a
   small training set of your choosing (2 inputs, at least one misclassified example per
   update). Verify your hand-traced final weights match running your Lab 13 `Perceptron` class
   on the same data and learning rate.
3. **(Bag-of-Words, 15 pts)** Given 4 short documents (provided by the instructor), build a
   shared vocabulary and compute each document's bag-of-words vector by hand; verify with your
   Lab 14 implementation.
4. **(Survey Synthesis, 20 pts)** In 1–2 paragraphs, explain how a decision tree (Week 12), a
   perceptron (Week 13), and a bag-of-words classifier (Week 14) each represent "a rule for
   classifying an example," and how each differs from the logic-based rules (Horn clauses,
   Week 7) earlier in the course.
5. **(Ethics Essay, 25 pts)** In 300–500 words, discuss a real or plausible scenario where a
   machine-learning-based system (not necessarily from this course) caused or risked a bias,
   safety, privacy, or societal-impact harm. Identify the specific failure mode and propose one
   concrete mitigation a development team could have applied before deployment.
