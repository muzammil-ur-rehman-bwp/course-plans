# Quiz 1 — Introduction to AI & Agents (Week 2)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Name two of the four historical "views" of AI (e.g., thinking humanly, acting rationally) and
   briefly describe one. (2 pts)
2. Write a PEAS specification (one item each for Performance measure, Environment, Actuators,
   Sensors) for a simple home thermostat. (3 pts)
3. What is the difference between the "agent function" and the "agent program"? (2 pts)
4. Classify chess as fully or partially observable, and as deterministic or stochastic. Justify
   each in one sentence. (2 pts)
5. Give one example of a utility-based agent and explain why a simple reflex agent would not be
   sufficient for it. (1 pt)
