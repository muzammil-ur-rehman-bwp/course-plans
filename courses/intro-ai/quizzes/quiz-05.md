# Quiz 5 — Probability & Bayesian Networks (Week 11)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State Bayes' rule in terms of `P(A|B)`, `P(B|A)`, `P(A)`, and `P(B)`. (2 pts)
2. In a diagnostic-test scenario, what does "base-rate neglect" mean, and why does it lead to
   overestimating `P(disease | positive test)`? (2 pts)
3. What does it mean for two events to be conditionally independent given a third event `C`?
   (2 pts)
4. In a Bayesian network, what determines which conditional probability table (CPT) a node
   needs? (1 pt)
5. Briefly describe, in 1–2 sentences, what "inference by enumeration" computes and why
   normalization is necessary. (2 pts)
