# Quiz 4 — Logic Inference & First-Order Logic (Week 8)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. What does it mean for a knowledge base `KB` to entail a sentence `α` (`KB ⊨ α`)? (2 pts)
2. In resolution refutation, why do we negate the query before adding it to the clause set?
   (2 pts)
3. What is a Horn clause (definite clause), and name the two chaining strategies that work on
   Horn-clause knowledge bases. (2 pts)
4. Translate "All birds can fly" into first-order logic using `Bird(x)` and `CanFly(x)`. (2 pts)
5. Explain, in one or two sentences, why `∀x ∃y Likes(x, y)` means something different from
   `∃y ∀x Likes(x, y)`. (2 pts)
