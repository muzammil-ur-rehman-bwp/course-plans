# Quiz 2 — Search: Uninformed & Informed (Week 4)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. What is the time complexity of BFS in terms of branching factor `b` and depth of the
   shallowest goal `d`? (1 pt)
2. Why is DFS not optimal in general, even though it is complete on a finite state space with a
   visited set? (2 pts)
3. Define admissibility of a heuristic function in one sentence. (2 pts)
4. Given `g(n) = 7` and `h(n) = 4` for a node `n`, what is `f(n)` under A*? (1 pt)
5. Explain, in 2–3 sentences, why simulated annealing can escape a local optimum that hill
   climbing cannot. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
