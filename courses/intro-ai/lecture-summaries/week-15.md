# Week 15 Summary — Computer Vision & Robotics Overview + AI Ethics

**Key takeaways:**
- Computer vision's core challenge is recovering structure/meaning from raw pixel arrays; edge
  detection (finding large local intensity changes) is a simple, concrete illustration.
- Robotics' core challenge is the sense-plan-act cycle in a dynamic, continuous, partially
  observable world; reactive control and planning (Weeks 3–4, 9) are complementary tools.
- AI ethics raises four recurring concerns across both classical and learned systems: bias and
  fairness, safety, privacy, and societal impact/accountability.

**You should now be able to:** implement a minimal edge-detection operation and a reactive
grid-world controller; identify which ethical concern (bias, safety, privacy, societal impact)
is most relevant to a given AI deployment scenario.
