# Week 9 Summary — Midterm Exam + Classical Planning

**Key takeaways:**
- The midterm covered Weeks 1–8 material under exam conditions.
- STRIPS represents an action by its preconditions, add-list, and delete-list, making action
  applicability and effects explicit rather than hidden inside a problem-specific function.
- A plan can be found by searching over states reachable via STRIPS actions — the same
  search algorithms from Weeks 3–4, now applied to a structured action representation.

**You should now be able to:** check whether a STRIPS action is applicable in a given state;
compute the resulting state; implement a small forward state-space planner for a toy domain.

**Next week:** uncertainty and probability — joint/conditional probability, the product rule,
and Bayes' rule.
