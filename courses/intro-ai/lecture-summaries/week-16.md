# Week 16 Summary — Capstone Presentations + Course Review

**Key takeaways:**
- The course built a layered map of classical AI: foundations and agents, search, logic,
  planning, uncertainty, and a brief survey of ML/NN/NLP/vision/robotics, closing with ethics.
- Recurring themes across modules: formulate the problem precisely before solving it; correctness
  conditions (admissibility, soundness, conditional-independence assumptions) determine whether
  an algorithm's output can be trusted; structure (heuristics, Horn clauses, STRIPS schemas,
  Bayesian networks) is what makes otherwise-intractable problems tractable.
- Classical, symbolic AI and modern, statistical AI are complementary pillars, not rivals.

**You should now be able to:** explain how each module's techniques relate to the others;
situate this course's classical/survey treatment relative to the curriculum's applied-ML and
deep-learning courses; present and defend a small AI project end-to-end.
