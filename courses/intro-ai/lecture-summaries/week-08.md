# Week 8 Summary — First-Order Logic; Midterm Review

**Key takeaways:**
- First-order logic adds constants, predicates, functions, variables, and quantifiers (∀, ∃) to
  propositional logic, letting us express general facts about objects and relations.
- Quantifier order and scope change meaning significantly — "every x has some y" is not the same
  claim as "some y works for every x."
- The midterm (Week 9) covers Weeks 1–8: AI foundations, agents, search (uninformed/informed/
  adversarial), and logic (propositional and first-order).

**You should now be able to:** state FOL syntax; translate English sentences to FOL and back;
recognize and avoid quantifier-scope ambiguity.
