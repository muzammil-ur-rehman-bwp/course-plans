# Week 13 Summary — Introduction to Neural Networks (Survey)

**Key takeaways:**
- A perceptron computes a weighted sum plus bias, then a step activation; the perceptron
  learning rule nudges weights toward correctly classifying misclassified examples.
- The perceptron converges only if the data is linearly separable — AND/OR are, XOR is not.
- Multi-layer networks overcome the linear-separability limit; the 2010s deep learning
  resurgence was driven by more data, GPU compute, and architectural/algorithmic refinements,
  not a single breakthrough idea.

**You should now be able to:** implement and train a perceptron from scratch; explain why XOR
defeats a single perceptron; describe, at a high level, why deeper networks and more
data/compute enabled modern deep learning.
