# Lab Assignment 15 — Capstone Work Session (Graded)

**Weight:** part of the weekly Lab Work component (15% of course grade, averaged across all
labs). This lab's grade also feeds into the Research Capstone's milestone tracking (see
`assignments/capstone-rubric.md`).

## Deliverable
Submit your capstone written-paper draft (Task C) and a short written record of the peer
feedback received during the practice talk (Task D), per `lab-manuals/lab-15.md`.

## Grading Rubric
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Task A: literature review finalized with an explicit, specific gap/question | 3 |
| Task B: experiment executed and results/outputs saved | 3 |
| Task C: written-paper draft covers all required sections (introduction, related work, method, results, limitations) | 2 |
| Task D: practice talk given; specific, actionable feedback recorded | 2 |
| **Total** | **10** |

## Notes
- This lab is process-graded (completion and quality of the milestone work), distinct from the
  Research Capstone's own final rubric, which grades the finished literature review, experiment,
  paper, and presentation in Week 16.
- Partial credit given for a good-faith draft with gaps; no credit for a submission showing no
  real progress since topic approval.
