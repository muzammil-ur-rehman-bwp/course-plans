# Quiz 2 — Description Logics in Depth (Week 4)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the ALC tableau's ⊔-rule (disjunction rule) precisely, including what makes it
   different from the ⊓-rule in terms of search. (2 pts)
2. What does it mean for a tableau branch to "clash," and what does a clash tell you about that
   branch? (2 pts)
3. State the complexity-class result for ALC concept satisfiability precisely (not just "it's
   hard"). (2 pts)
4. A biomedical terminology ontology needs to classify hundreds of thousands of concepts in
   polynomial time but does not need negation or disjunction. Which OWL 2 profile fits, and why?
   (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "since ALC satisfiability is decidable, checking it must be fast in
   practice for any-size ontology." Identify what is wrong with this claim. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
