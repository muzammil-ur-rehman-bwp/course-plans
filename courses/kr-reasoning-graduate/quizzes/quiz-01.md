# Quiz 1 — Modal Logic (Week 2)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the satisfaction clause for □φ at a world w in a Kripke model ⟨W, R, V⟩. (2 pts)
2. Which single property of the accessibility relation R is both necessary and sufficient to
   validate the axiom schema □φ→φ at every world? (2 pts)
3. Given R is reflexive and transitive but not symmetric, does the model validate axiom 5
   (◇φ→□◇φ)? Answer yes/no and justify in one sentence. (2 pts)
4. Under the epistemic reading (□ as "agent a knows"), why is an equivalence relation the
   natural choice for indistinguishability between worlds? (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "if R has no outgoing edges from world w, then □False is true at w, so the
   model must be inconsistent." Identify what is wrong with this claim. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
