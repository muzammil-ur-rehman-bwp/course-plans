# Quiz 6 — Multi-Agent Epistemic Logic & Ontology Engineering (Weeks 12–13)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Give the Kripke-model construction (in terms of individual accessibility relations R_i) for
   common knowledge C_G, and for distributed knowledge D_G. (2 pts)
2. In the muddy-children puzzle with k muddy children, at which round do the muddy children first
   answer "yes," and why not earlier? (2 pts)
3. What is a competency question, and what role does it play in the ontology-development cycle?
   (2 pts)
4. Name one failure mode of purely lexical ontology alignment, with a concrete example pair of
   class names. (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "the muddy children's father's announcement gave them new factual
   information about who is muddy." Identify what is wrong with this claim. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
