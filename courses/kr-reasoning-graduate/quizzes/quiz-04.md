# Quiz 4 — Belief Revision & Argumentation (Weeks 7–8)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State AGM postulate (K∗2) (success) and explain in one sentence why an operator that ignores
   φ entirely would violate it. (2 pts)
2. In Dalal revision, K∗φ's models are chosen by what criterion among φ's models? (2 pts)
3. Explain, in one or two sentences, the difference between belief revision and belief update —
   what question does each answer? (2 pts)
4. Define "S is admissible" for a Dung argumentation framework, using the terms conflict-free and
   defends. (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "the grounded extension always equals the union of all preferred
   extensions." Identify what is wrong with this claim, using the mutual-attack example as a
   counterexample. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
