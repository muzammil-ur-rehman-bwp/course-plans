# Quiz 5 — Knowledge Graphs & KG Reasoning (Weeks 10–11)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the TransE modeling intuition (the "translation" idea) and its scoring function.
   (2 pts)
2. What is a "corrupted" triple used for in TransE training, and how is it typically generated?
   (2 pts)
3. Define "confidence" for a mined closed-path rule, in terms of support and body count. (2 pts)
4. Give one concrete reason a symbolic rule might be preferred over an embedding score for a
   given candidate fact, and one concrete reason the reverse might be true. (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "a knowledge-graph embedding's high score for a candidate fact is proof
   that fact is true." Identify what is wrong with this claim. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
