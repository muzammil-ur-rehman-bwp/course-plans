# Week 12 Summary — Multi-Agent Epistemic Reasoning

**Key takeaways:**
- Everyone-knows E_G is modeled by the union of individual accessibility relations; common
  knowledge C_G by its transitive closure (reachability by any finite chain of individual
  steps); distributed knowledge D_G by the intersection — pooling information can yield
  knowledge no individual agent has.
- In the muddy-children puzzle, every child answers "no" for k−1 rounds and every muddy child
  answers "yes" simultaneously at round k; the public announcement's power lies in making "at
  least one is muddy" *common* knowledge, not in conveying new factual content.
- This week's epistemic lens (what agents know/believe) is explicitly distinct from *Artificial
  Intelligence* Graduate's game-theoretic, payoff-based lens on multi-agent systems.

**You should now be able to:** state the Kripke constructions for E_G, C_G, D_G; construct and
simulate the muddy-children puzzle's public-announcement update; distinguish this week's
knowledge-based multi-agent lens from a strategic/game-theoretic one.
