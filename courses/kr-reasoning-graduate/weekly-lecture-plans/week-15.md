# Week 15 Lecture Plan — Knowledge Representation and Reasoning (Graduate)
## Topic: Research Methods and Project Work Session

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour lab/seminar

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Critique a short KR paper excerpt using a structured framework (claimed result, argument
   adequacy, example representativeness, formalism comparison). (*Analyze*)
2. Give and receive structured, specific feedback on a capstone work-in-progress or Paper
   Critique presentation. (*Evaluate*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | How to read/critique a KR paper | Abstract → results/examples → formal definitions → related work → full read; structured critique framework |
| 0:30–1:00 | In-class critique exercise | Short paper excerpt, structured group critique |
| 1:00–2:00 | Paper Critique presentations | Student presentations (continues into lab/seminar block as needed) |
| 2:00–3:00 (lab) | Capstone work session | Instructor-guided literature review/experiment work time |

### Materials/Equipment
- Handout: "How to Read and Critique a KR Paper" framework
- Short paper excerpt for the in-class exercise
- Sign-up slots for Paper Critique presentations

### Formative Check (in-class)
Structured group critique of the short excerpt: identify the claimed formal result, whether the
paper's argument actually establishes it, and how it compares to a formalism covered this
semester.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 15: structured capstone work session (literature review, experiment design/execution,
written-paper drafting) with instructor feedback (see `lab-manuals/lab-15.md`). Paper Critique &
Presentation due this week.
