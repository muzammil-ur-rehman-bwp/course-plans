# Week 16 Lecture Plan — Knowledge Representation and Reasoning (Graduate)
## Topic: Capstone Research Presentations; Course Review

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour lab/seminar (both largely devoted to presentations)

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Present an original research-style capstone in conference-talk format and field audience
   questions accurately. (*Evaluate*, *Create*)
2. Recap and connect the course's full map of advanced KR formalisms, from modal/temporal logic
   through explainability, back to the undergraduate foundations and forward to current
   research. (*Evaluate*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Introduction | Presentation order, timing/Q&A norms reminder |
| 0:10–1:50 | Capstone presentations | Student/pair presentations (7–10 min + Q&A each; continues into lab/seminar block as needed for the full roster) |
| 1:50–2:00 | Course review | Course map recap; connections forward to current KR&R research |

### Materials/Equipment
- Presentation schedule
- Capstone rubric (for instructor scoring during presentations)
- Slides: "Course Review — From Modal Logic to Explainability"

### Formative Check (in-class)
Peer evaluation: for each presentation, audience members note one strength and one specific
question, submitted anonymously to the presenter after their talk.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Capstone final paper submission + presentation (graded per `assignments/capstone-rubric.md`). No
new lab assigned this week.
