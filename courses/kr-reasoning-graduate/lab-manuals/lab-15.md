# Lab Manual 15 — Capstone Work Session

**Duration:** 3 hours | **Prerequisite:** Week 15 lecture; capstone topic approved since Week
7–8

## Objectives
Use structured, instructor-guided time to finalize the capstone literature review, run the
small reproduced/extended experiment, and draft the written paper ahead of Week 16's
presentations.

## Setup
1. Bring your approved capstone proposal and whatever code/notebook you have built so far.
2. Open (or create) `capstone_notebook.ipynb` or your project's working file.

## Procedure
1. **Task A — Literature review finalization:** for each of your 3–5 papers, confirm your
   written summary states problem/method/result accurately, and confirm the review ends with an
   explicit, specific gap or question (not a generic "more research is needed").
2. **Task B — Experiment execution:** run (or re-run, with any Week 15 feedback incorporated)
   your reproduced/extended experiment; save all results/plots/output needed for the written
   paper and presentation.
3. **Task C — Written-paper draft:** produce a draft covering introduction, related work,
   method, results, and limitations, in conference-short-paper structure.
4. **Task D — Practice talk:** give a 5–7 minute practice run of your capstone talk to a partner
   or small group; receive and record at least 2 pieces of specific, actionable feedback.

## Expected Output
A capstone written-paper draft (per Task C) and a short written record of the Task D feedback
received, both ready to incorporate before Week 16.

## Submission
Submit the Task C written-paper draft via the course submission system by the end of this
session, alongside the Paper Critique & Presentation deliverable due this week (see
`assignments/paper-critique-and-presentation.md`).
