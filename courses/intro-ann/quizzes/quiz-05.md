# Quiz 5 — Regularization & Frameworks (Week 12)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. What is the effect of L2 regularization on the weight update rule, and what is the colloquial
   name for this effect? (2 pts)
2. Explain the difference between dropout's behavior during training vs. at test/evaluation
   time. (2 pts)
3. What does `optimizer.zero_grad()` do in a PyTorch training loop, and what happens if it is
   omitted? (2 pts)
4. Why does a framework's `nn.CrossEntropyLoss` typically expect raw logits rather than
   softmax-ed probabilities as its input? (2 pts)
5. Name one validation-set-based technique, covered this semester, for deciding when to stop
   training. (2 pts)
