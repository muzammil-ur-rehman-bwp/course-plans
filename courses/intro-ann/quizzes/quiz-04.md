# Quiz 4 — Initialization & Optimizers (Week 10)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Why does initializing all weights in a layer to zero prevent that layer from learning useful,
   distinct features? (2 pts)
2. What is the key difference between Xavier/Glorot initialization and He initialization, and
   which activation function is each designed for? (2 pts)
3. Write Adam's first- and second-moment update equations ($m_t$ and $v_t$). (2 pts)
4. Why is bias correction ($\hat m_t, \hat v_t$) necessary in Adam, especially early in training?
   (2 pts)
5. Name one advantage of mini-batch gradient descent with momentum over plain SGD. (2 pts)
