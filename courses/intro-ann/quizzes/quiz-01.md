# Quiz 1 — Perceptron & Linear Separability (Week 3)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Write the perceptron's prediction formula given weights $\mathbf{w}$, bias $b$, and input
   $\mathbf{x}$. (2 pts)
2. State the perceptron learning rule's weight update and explain, in one sentence, what happens
   to the update when a prediction is already correct. (2 pts)
3. Is the dataset $\{((0,0),0), ((1,1),0), ((0,1),1), ((1,0),1)\}$ linearly separable? Justify
   your answer in one or two sentences. (2 pts)
4. Why does a single perceptron fail to learn XOR, even given unlimited training epochs? (2 pts)
5. Name one activation function covered in Week 3 that does **not** saturate for large positive
   inputs, and state its formula. (2 pts)

before the quiz).**
