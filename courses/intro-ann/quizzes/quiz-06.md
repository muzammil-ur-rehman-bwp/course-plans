# Quiz 6 — CNNs, RNNs, and Model Debugging (Week 15)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. What is weight sharing in a convolutional layer, and why does it make CNNs more
   parameter-efficient than an MLP on image data? (2 pts)
2. Why do plain RNNs struggle to learn dependencies across long sequences, and what structural
   change in an LSTM mitigates this? (2 pts)
3. Given a loss curve where training loss keeps decreasing but validation loss starts rising
   partway through training, what is the diagnosis and what is one fix? (2 pts)
4. List, in the cheapest-check-first order given in lecture, three things to check when a
   training loss is completely flat and never decreases. (2 pts)
5. Why must hyperparameter selection be based on validation performance rather than test
   performance? (2 pts)
