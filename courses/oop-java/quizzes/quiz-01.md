# Quiz 1 — Encapsulation (Week 2)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. What is the risk of making a field `public` instead of `private` with a getter/setter? (2 pts)
2. What does a setter typically do that a plain public field assignment cannot? (2 pts)
3. Inside a constructor, what does `this.x = x;` do differently from `x = x;`, and why does the
   difference matter? (2 pts)
4. What makes a class immutable, and name one practical benefit of immutability. (2 pts)
5. Given the following, what is wrong with this class's design from an encapsulation standpoint?
   ```java
   public class Thermostat {
       public double targetTemp;
   }
   ```
   (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
