# Week 15 Summary — Research Proposal Work Session

**Key takeaways:**
- This week is dedicated drafting/feedback time for the capstone's four required components.
- The thesis-committee-style peer-feedback protocol asks four questions: is the problem statement
  precise and open; does the survey accurately represent and motivate from the cited papers; is
  the approach genuinely novel; is the feasibility argument honest about its main risk.
- Feedback should be triaged with reasoned agreement or disagreement, and translated into a
  specific, actionable revision plan before the final submission.

**You should now be able to:** apply the four-question peer-review protocol to a full draft
proposal; triage received feedback; produce a specific, actionable revision plan.
