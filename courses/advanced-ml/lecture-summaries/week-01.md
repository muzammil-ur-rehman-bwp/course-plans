# Week 1 Summary — Postgraduate Overview: The Statistical-Learning-Theory Research Frontier

**Key takeaways:**
- This course assumes the entire graduate-ML toolkit (PAC/VC/Rademacher, concentration
  inequalities, convex optimization/KKT, kernels/RKHS, ensemble theory, Bayesian ML, CRFs,
  dimensionality-reduction theory, a conceptual causal-inference intro, model-selection theory) as
  settled background and does not re-teach it.
- The course has five pillars: minimax/high-dimensional statistics, full-information online convex
  optimization, nonparametric Bayesian methods, causal inference in depth, and trustworthy/robust
  statistical learning.
- This course's full-information online convex optimization (Week 5) is a genuinely different
  setting from, and does not duplicate, *Advanced Artificial Intelligence*'s bandit/partial-
  information regret theory.
- A research proposal has four required components: a problem statement, a 5+ paper related-work
  survey, a proposed novel approach, and a feasibility argument or preliminary results.

**You should now be able to:** state what this course assumes versus covers; map a research
question onto one of the five pillars; state the four components of a research proposal.

parametric-estimation lower bound.
