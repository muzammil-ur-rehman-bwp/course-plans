# Week 16 Summary — Capstone Research Proposal Presentations + Course Review

**Key takeaways:**
- Capstone research-proposal presentations are defended in a qualifying-exam/thesis-proposal
  format, graded per `assignments/capstone-rubric.md`.
- The course's five pillars (minimax/high-dimensional statistics; full-information OCO;
  nonparametric Bayes; causal inference in depth; trustworthy/robust statistical learning) each
  generalize a specific piece of the graduate course's assumed toolkit.
- This course's content connects to, and is deliberately non-overlapping with, the sibling
  postgraduate AI, ANN, DL, and KR&R courses.

**You should now be able to:** defend an original research proposal under committee-style
questioning; situate every result covered this semester within the course's five-pillar map; state
precisely how this course's content connects to and differs from its sibling postgraduate courses.
