# Week 14 Summary — Current Open Problems Survey

**Key takeaways:**
- This is a fast-moving area; the instructor refreshes the specific current papers each offering.
- Three illustrative open-question shapes: statistical–computational gaps in high-dimensional
  structured estimation; the contested question of how (or whether) to relax fairness criteria
  under Week 11's impossibility result; and the statistical–computational tradeoff in scaling
  robust-statistics guarantees to modern high-dimensional learning.
- A genuinely open problem has a clear best-known upper bound/positive result, a clear best-known
  lower bound/negative result or partial characterization, and a named, specific obstruction
  explaining the persisting gap — a diagnostic directly useful for evaluating a capstone problem
  statement.

**You should now be able to:** state what is known and not known for a surveyed open question;
identify the specific obstruction keeping it open; relate an open-question shape to your own
emerging capstone interest.
