# Quiz 3 — Full-Information OCO & the Dirichlet Process (Week 7)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the full-information OCO protocol and explain, in one sentence, how it differs from the
   bandit setting. (2 pts)
2. Write online gradient descent's update rule and its regret bound $\mathrm{Regret}_T\leq
   DG\sqrt T$. (2 pts)
3. State the Dirichlet process's defining property in terms of finite partitions. (2 pts)
4. Write the stick-breaking weight formula $\pi_k$ in terms of $\beta_1,\dots,\beta_k$, and state
   the distribution each $\beta_k$ is drawn from. (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "Since FTRL and online gradient descent both achieve $O(\sqrt T)$ regret,
   they must produce identical sequences of played points on any loss sequence." Identify what is
   wrong with this claim. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
