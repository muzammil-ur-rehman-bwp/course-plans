# Quiz 6 — Distribution Shift & Algorithmic Fairness (Week 12)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the covariate-shift assumption and the importance-weighted risk identity. (2 pts)
2. Explain, in one sentence, why importance weighting's guarantee degrades when overlap between
   $P_{\mathrm{tr}}(x)$ and $P_{\mathrm{te}}(x)$ is poor. (2 pts)
3. State the formal definitions of equalized odds and calibration/predictive parity. (2 pts)
4. State the PPV identity $\mathrm{PPV}=p\cdot\mathrm{TPR}/(p\cdot\mathrm{TPR}+(1-p)\cdot
   \mathrm{FPR})$ and explain why it is monotonic in the base rate $p$ for a non-degenerate
   classifier. (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "A sufficiently sophisticated classifier can always be tuned to satisfy
   both equalized odds and calibration across groups." Identify precisely when this claim is
   false. (2 pts)

before the quiz).**
