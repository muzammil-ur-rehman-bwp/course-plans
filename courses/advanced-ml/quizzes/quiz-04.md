# Quiz 4 — The Chinese Restaurant Process & Potential Outcomes (Week 9)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the Chinese Restaurant Process's seating rule precisely. (2 pts)
2. State what it means for the CRP's induced partition to be exchangeable, and explain in one
   sentence why this matters for valid Bayesian inference. (2 pts)
3. State the fundamental problem of causal inference using potential-outcomes notation. (2 pts)
4. State the unconfoundedness and overlap assumptions precisely. (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "If unconfoundedness holds, the naive difference-in-means estimator
   already equals the ATE, so propensity-score weighting is unnecessary." Identify what is wrong
   with this claim. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
