# Quiz 2 — High-Dimensional Statistics I & II (Week 6)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the sub-Gaussian MGF definition and the resulting tail bound. (2 pts)
2. Explain why a centered $\chi^2_1$ variable is sub-exponential but not sub-Gaussian. (2 pts)
3. State Bernstein's inequality's two-regime tail bound and identify the crossover point between
   the two regimes. (2 pts)
4. State the Marchenko–Pastur law's support interval in terms of $\gamma=p/n$. (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "If the true covariance is the identity, the sample covariance's
   eigenvalues should all be close to 1 as long as $n$ is reasonably large." Identify what is
   wrong with this claim when $p$ is comparable to $n$. (2 pts)

before the quiz).**
