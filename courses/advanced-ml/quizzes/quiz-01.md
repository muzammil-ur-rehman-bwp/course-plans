# Quiz 1 — Minimax Lower Bounds (Week 4)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the minimax risk $M_n$ precisely, and explain in one sentence why an upper bound on one
   estimator's risk alone never certifies that estimator as rate-optimal. (2 pts)
2. State Fano's inequality. (2 pts)
3. List the three steps of the minimax-lower-bound recipe (reduction to testing; packing-set
   construction; information bound via KL divergence). (2 pts)
4. For the Gaussian location family 2-point packing set $\{-\epsilon,+\epsilon\}$, state the KL
   divergence $\mathrm{KL}(\mathcal N(-\epsilon,1/n)\|\mathcal N(\epsilon,1/n))$ and the resulting
   critical separation scale $\epsilon^\star$ in terms of $n$. (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "A tighter minimax lower bound can always be obtained simply by using a
   larger packing set $M$." Identify what is wrong with this claim, with reference to the Week 2
   in-class exercise's 4-point example. (2 pts)

before the quiz).**
