# Week 14 Lecture Plan — Advanced Machine Learning (Post Graduate)
## Topic: Current Open Problems Survey

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour research seminar/lab (critical-writing/discussion format)

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. State what is currently known and not known for 2–3 active open problems. (*Understand*)
2. Explain why each surveyed question remains open (a genuine technical obstruction). (*Evaluate*)
3. Relate a surveyed open question to one's own emerging capstone interest. (*Evaluate*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Framing | This is a fast-moving area; today's specific papers refresh each offering |
| 0:10–0:40 | Open question 1 | Statistical/computational gaps in high-dimensional structured estimation |
| 0:40–1:10 | Open question 2 | Relaxing fairness criteria under the Week 11 impossibility result |
| 1:10–1:40 | Open question 3 | Scaling robust-statistics guarantees to modern high-dimensional learning |
| 1:40–2:00 | Synthesis | What makes a question genuinely open vs. merely unexplored |

### Materials/Equipment
- Instructor-assigned current NeurIPS/ICML/COLT papers (refreshed each offering)
- Slides: "Open Problems at the Statistical-Learning-Theory Frontier"

### Formative Check (in-class)
For one surveyed open question, state the best known upper bound, the best known lower bound (or
partial result), and identify the specific obstruction preventing them from matching.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 14: a frontier-paper mini-investigation applying the Week 13 problem-statement-extraction
skill to a current assigned paper (see `lab-manuals/lab-14.md`).
