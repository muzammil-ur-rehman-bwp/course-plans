# Week 15 Lecture Plan — Advanced Machine Learning (Post Graduate)
## Topic: Research Proposal Work Session

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour research seminar/lab (workshop format)

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Draft all four required components of the capstone proposal. (*Apply, Create*)
2. Apply the thesis-committee-style peer-feedback protocol to a peer's draft. (*Analyze, Evaluate*)
3. Produce an explicit, specific revision plan from feedback received. (*Evaluate, Create*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Framing | Today is dedicated drafting/feedback time, not new lecture content |
| 0:15–0:30 | The four-question peer-review protocol | Precision; survey accuracy/synthesis; genuine novelty; honest feasibility |
| 0:30–2:00 (into lab) | Draft exchange & structured peer review | Paired peer review using the four-question protocol |
| — | Paper Critique & Presentation | In-class presentations during this session |

### Materials/Equipment
- Peer-review worksheet (four-question protocol)
- Students' current capstone proposal drafts

### Formative Check (in-class)
As a reviewer, spot-check one cited paper in your assigned peer's survey against the paper itself
and report whether the peer's summary accurately represents it.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 15: capstone peer-review workshop — give and receive structured feedback and produce a
revision plan (see `lab-manuals/lab-15.md`).

### Deliverables Due This Week
- Draft capstone proposal (all four required components).
- Paper Critique & Presentation: written critique due; in-class presentation this session.
