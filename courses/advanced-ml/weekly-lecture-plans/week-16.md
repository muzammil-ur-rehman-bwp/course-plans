# Week 16 Lecture Plan — Advanced Machine Learning (Post Graduate)
## Topic: Capstone Research Proposal Presentations + Course Review

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour research seminar/lab (presentation/defense format; no lab
this week — capstone presentations replace the lab session)

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Present and defend an original research proposal under committee-style questioning.
   (*Evaluate, Create*)
2. Recap and synthesize the course's full theoretical map across all five pillars. (*Understand,
   Evaluate*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–3:30 | Capstone defenses | Each student presents (12–15 min) + committee-style Q&A, per `presentations/capstone-presentation-template.md` |

| 4:00–5:00 | Forward pointers | Where this course's foundation connects into *Advanced AI*, *Advanced ANN*, *Advanced DL* |
