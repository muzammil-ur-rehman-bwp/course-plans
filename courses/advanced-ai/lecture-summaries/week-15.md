# Week 15 Summary — Research Proposal Work Session

**Key takeaways:**
- No new technical material this week; the focus is converting course-long preparation into a
  complete, defensible draft proposal.
- Peer feedback this week is modeled on a thesis-committee's critical questioning across four
  dimensions: problem-statement precision, survey accuracy/synthesis, genuine novelty of the
  proposed approach, and honesty of the feasibility argument.
- A concrete, itemized revision plan — not just receiving feedback — is what the week requires.

**You should now be able to:** give and receive structured, committee-style critical feedback on
a proposal draft; produce a specific revision plan in response.
