# Week 13 Summary — Research Methods at the Postgraduate Level

**Key takeaways:**
- A problem statement must be precise and falsifiable: a reader should be able to state what
  evidence would resolve it, just from the statement itself.
- A postgraduate related-work survey must accurately represent each paper, relate papers to each
  other, and use the synthesis to motivate a specific, stated gap.
- A feasibility argument must name the main technical risk honestly, not just list reasons for
  optimism; postgraduate work is judged on the soundness of a research plan, not on completed
  results.

**You should now be able to:** write and pressure-test a problem statement; apply the three-part
related-work survey standard; construct a feasibility argument's skeleton.
