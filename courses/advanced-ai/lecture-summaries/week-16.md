# Week 16 Summary — Capstone Research Proposal Presentations; Course Review

**Key takeaways:**
- Capstone defenses follow a qualifying-exam/thesis-proposal-defense format: problem statement,
  related work, proposed approach, feasibility argument or preliminary results, risks, and
  committee-style Q&A.
- A strong defense answer honestly names unresolved risk rather than overstating certainty.
- The course's four pillars (regret/bandits; multi-agent systems/game theory; AI safety/
  alignment/interpretability; foundational debates/research methods) connect forward into the
  sibling postgraduate courses and into an actual thesis trajectory.

**You should now be able to:** defend a complete research proposal under critical questioning;
situate this course's four pillars relative to future thesis work.

**Course complete.** Next steps: the Final Exam (Week 17, comprehensive, emphasis on Weeks
9–14) and, beyond this course, a thesis or dissertation proposal built on these foundations.
