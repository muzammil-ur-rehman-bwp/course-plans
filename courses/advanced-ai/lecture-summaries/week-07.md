# Week 7 Summary — Algorithmic Game Theory II: Mechanism Design

**Key takeaways:**
- Mechanism design chooses allocation and payment rules so that self-interested agents'
  equilibrium behavior produces a desired social outcome.
- The VCG mechanism picks the welfare-maximizing outcome and charges each agent the externality
  it imposes on others; this makes truthful reporting a dominant strategy, proven by decomposing
  each agent's utility into a term the mechanism maximizes plus a term independent of their
  report.
- The second-price (Vickrey) auction is VCG's single-item special case; ad auctions and resource
  allocation borrow from (and deliberately depart from) this blueprint in practice.

**You should now be able to:** derive and apply the VCG payment rule; reproduce its truthfulness
proof; implement and empirically verify a VCG mechanism's truthfulness.
