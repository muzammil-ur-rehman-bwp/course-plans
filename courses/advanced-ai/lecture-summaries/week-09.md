# Week 9 Summary — AI Safety and Alignment II

**Key takeaways:**
- Reward modeling learns a reward function from human pairwise preferences (a Bradley-Terry-
  style logistic model) rather than requiring a hand-specified reward, narrowing but not closing
  the outer-alignment gap.
- Scalable oversight (debate, iterated amplification) targets the problem of evaluating AI
  outputs on tasks too hard for direct human judgment.
- Interpretability is framed as a candidate safety tool: inspecting internals directly could, in
  principle, catch inner-alignment failures that behavior alone would not reveal.

**You should now be able to:** fit a simple preference-based reward model; state one concrete
limitation of reward modeling; describe debate/amplification's general mechanism.

**Next week:** Interpretability research — feature attribution, mechanistic interpretability,
and the gap between post-hoc explanation and genuine understanding.
