# Week 1 Summary — Postgraduate Overview: The AI Research Frontier

**Key takeaways:**
- This course assumes graduate AI (advanced search, game theory, SAT/SMT, planning complexity,
  MDPs/POMDPs/RL, AI complexity, research methods) as firm background, not re-taught material.
- Four pillars structure the semester: regret theory/bandits, multi-agent RL/algorithmic game
  theory, AI safety/alignment/interpretability, and foundational/philosophical debates.
- A research proposal has four components — problem statement, related-work survey, proposed
  approach, feasibility argument/preliminary results — introduced now and developed in Week 13.

**You should now be able to:** map a research question onto this course's four pillars; identify
which graduate-AI topics a given question assumes without re-deriving; state the four components
of a research proposal.

**Next week:** Online learning and regret minimization — the multiplicative-weights algorithm
and its regret bound, derived from first principles.
