# Lab Assignment 15 — Capstone Peer-Review Workshop (Graded)

**Weight:** part of the weekly Lab Work component (10% of course grade, averaged across all labs).

## Deliverable
Submit `lab15_peer_review.md` containing Tasks A–D from `lab-manuals/lab-15.md`.

## Grading Rubric
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Task A complete (draft exchanged; peer's draft received and reviewed) | 1 |
| Task B complete (all four committee-style questions answered with specific justification, including a genuine spot-check) | 4 |
| Task C complete (received feedback triaged with reasoned agreement/disagreement per question) | 2 |
| Task D complete (revision plan names concrete, specific changes tied to identified weaknesses) | 3 |
| **Total** | **10** |

## Notes
- This lab's output is graded independently of the capstone draft it reviews — a thorough,
  specific review of a peer's weak draft scores well; a vague, encouraging review of a peer's
  strong draft does not.
- This lab feeds directly into the Week 15 capstone draft-proposal deliverable
  (`assignments/capstone-proposal-guidelines.md`) and the Week 16 defense
  (`presentations/capstone-presentation-template.md`).
