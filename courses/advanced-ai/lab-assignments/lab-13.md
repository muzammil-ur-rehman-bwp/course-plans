# Lab Assignment 13 — Problem Statement Drafting & Related-Work Annotation (Graded)

**Weight:** part of the weekly Lab Work component (10% of course grade, averaged across all labs).

## Deliverable
Submit `lab13_proposal_workshop.md` containing Tasks A–D from `lab-manuals/lab-13.md`.

## Grading Rubric
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Task A complete (a genuine, specific one-paragraph problem-statement draft) | 2 |
| Task B complete (peer critique received and recorded verbatim, applying the precision test) | 2 |
| Task C complete (revision made with a specific, traceable response to the critique) | 2 |
| Task D complete (5+ papers annotated using all three parts of the postgraduate standard) | 4 |
| **Total** | **10** |

## Notes
- This lab's work feeds directly into the capstone (`assignments/capstone-proposal-guidelines.md`)
  — it is graded as a milestone in its own right, not merely as capstone preparation.
- An annotation that only restates a paper's title or abstract, without stating what the paper
  actually found or how it relates to the student's stated gap, cannot receive full credit on
  Task D.
