# Lab Assignment 12 — Position Paper: The Chinese Room & Understanding (Graded)

**Weight:** part of the weekly Lab Work component (10% of course grade, averaged across all labs).

## Deliverable
Submit `lab12_position_paper.md` (or `.pdf`) containing Tasks A–D from `lab-manuals/lab-12.md`.

## Grading Rubric
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Task A complete (argument reconstructed precisely; each rebuttal correctly matched to its target premise) | 3 |
| Task B complete (a clear, one-paragraph position stated) | 1 |
| Task C complete (500–700-word defense; dependent rebuttal(s) and falsification condition stated explicitly) | 4 |
| Task D complete (a specific peer counterpoint engaged with a substantive rebuttal-response) | 2 |
| **Total** | **10** |

## Notes
- The position taken does not need to be the "mainstream" one — it is graded on precision,
  internal consistency, and how well it is defended against a genuine counterpoint, not on which
  side it takes.
- Mismatching a rebuttal to the wrong premise in Task A is treated as a factual error, not a
  matter of interpretation, and is graded accordingly.
