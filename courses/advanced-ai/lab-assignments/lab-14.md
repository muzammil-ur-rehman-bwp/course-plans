# Lab Assignment 14 — Frontier Paper Mini-Investigation (Graded)

**Weight:** part of the weekly Lab Work component (10% of course grade, averaged across all labs).

## Deliverable
Submit `lab14_frontier_brief.md` containing Tasks A–D from `lab-manuals/lab-14.md`.

## Grading Rubric
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Task A complete (full, correct citation recorded) | 1 |
| Task B complete (problem statement extracted in the student's own words and passes the precision test) | 3 |
| Task C complete (pillar correctly identified with a specific, concrete mechanism cited) | 3 |
| Task D complete (a specific open question stated; evidence-supported and speculative claims correctly distinguished and justified) | 3 |
| **Total** | **10** |

## Notes
- Because this week explicitly covers a fast-moving area, grading focuses on whether the
  student correctly applied the extraction/mapping/classification *skill*, not on whether their
  take on the paper matches any single "correct" interpretation.
- A brief that cannot name a specific section or result for each evidence-supported claim cannot
  receive full credit on Task D, regardless of how confidently the claim is stated.
