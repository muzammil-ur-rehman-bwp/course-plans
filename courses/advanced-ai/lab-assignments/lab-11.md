# Lab Assignment 11 — Position Paper: Symbol Grounding & the Frame Problem (Graded)

**Weight:** part of the weekly Lab Work component (10% of course grade, averaged across all labs).

## Deliverable
Submit `lab11_position_paper.md` (or `.pdf`) containing Tasks A–D from `lab-manuals/lab-11.md`.

## Grading Rubric
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Task A complete (both problems restated precisely, in the student's own words) | 2 |
| Task B complete (a specific design choice identified and concretely connected to one or both problems) | 2 |
| Task C complete (500–700-word position paper, with a clear, evaluated position and precise citations) | 4 |
| Task D complete (a specific peer counterpoint identified and a substantive rebuttal-response written) | 2 |
| **Total** | **10** |

## Notes
- The position taken does not need to be the "mainstream" one — it is graded on how precisely it
  is stated, how concretely it engages the chosen system, and how well it is defended, not on
  which side it takes.
- A paper that only summarizes Harnad (1990) or McCarthy & Hayes (1969) without applying the
  argument to the chosen modern system (Task B) cannot receive full credit on Task C, regardless
  of writing quality.
