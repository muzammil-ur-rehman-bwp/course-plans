# Lab Manual 15 — Capstone Peer-Review Workshop

**Duration:** 3 hours (peer-review workshop format) | **Prerequisite:** Week 15 lecture; a
current capstone proposal draft (problem statement, 5+ paper survey, proposed approach, and a
feasibility argument or preliminary result)

## Objectives
Apply the Week 15 thesis-committee-style peer-feedback protocol to a peer's capstone proposal
draft, receive and triage feedback on your own draft, and produce an explicit, specific revision
plan before the Week 15 draft deadline.

## Setup
No code environment is required this week. Bring your current capstone proposal draft (all four
required components, even if incomplete) and be prepared to exchange it with an assigned peer.
Create a single document `lab15_peer_review.md`.

## Procedure
1. **Task A — Draft exchange:** submit your current draft for peer review, and receive your
   assigned peer's draft.
2. **Task B — Structured review:** as reviewer, answer the four thesis-committee-style questions
   from Week 15, §2, in writing, for your assigned peer's draft, each with a specific
   justification (not a rating): (1) is the problem statement precise and genuinely open? (2)
   does the related-work survey correctly represent the cited papers and motivate the stated
   gap — spot-check at least one cited paper's summary against the paper itself; (3) is the
   proposed approach actually novel relative to the surveyed work; (4) is the feasibility
   argument honest about the main risk?
3. **Task C — Receive and triage feedback:** as author, read the feedback you receive. For each
   of the four questions, state in 1–2 sentences whether you agree with the critique and why.
4. **Task D — Revision plan:** write an explicit revision plan: for each weakness identified (by
   your reviewer, or by your own Task C triage), state the specific change you will make before
   the Week 15 draft deadline.

## Expected Output
A single document containing Tasks A–D as clearly labeled sections: the review you gave (Task
B), your triage of the review you received (Task C), and your resulting revision plan (Task D).

## Submission
Submit `lab15_peer_review.md` via the course submission system by the end of the lab session.
