# Lab Manual 14 — Frontier Paper Mini-Investigation

**Duration:** 3 hours (critical-writing/discussion format) | **Prerequisite:** Week 14 lecture

## Objectives
Practice the Week 13 problem-statement-extraction skill on a current, instructor-assigned
research paper, map it onto this course's pillars, and distinguish the claims it supports with
evidence from the claims it only speculates about — direct, deliberate rehearsal for identifying
your own capstone's problem statement and gap.

## Setup
No code environment is required this week, unless your assigned paper includes a small
accompanying demonstration you choose to reproduce. Obtain your instructor-assigned (or, with
approval, self-selected) current paper and its full citation. Create a single document
`lab14_frontier_brief.md`.

## Procedure
1. **Task A — Paper identification:** record the full, correct citation (title, authors, venue,
   year) of your assigned paper.
2. **Task B — Problem-statement extraction:** state the paper's actual problem statement in your
   own words — not its title or abstract verbatim. Apply the Week 13 precision test to your own
   extraction: does it state what evidence would resolve the paper's question?
3. **Task C — Pillar mapping:** identify which of this course's pillars or areas (regret/bandit
   theory; multi-agent RL/algorithmic game theory; AI safety and alignment; interpretability;
   foundational debates) the paper builds on, and explain concretely how, citing a specific
   mechanism or result from the relevant week.
4. **Task D — Open question and evidence/speculation split:** state one specific open question
   the paper leaves unresolved. List at least 2 claims the paper supports with direct evidence
   (cite the specific result or experiment) and at least 1 claim it only speculates about,
   justifying each classification.

## Expected Output
A single document containing Tasks A–D as clearly labeled sections.

## Submission
Submit `lab14_frontier_brief.md` via the course submission system by the end of the lab session.
