# Quiz 5 — Interpretability & the Symbol Grounding Problem (Week 12)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the key difference between feature attribution and mechanistic interpretability, as two
   different kinds of claims about a model. (2 pts)
2. Describe the randomization sanity check for an attribution method, and state what a high
   correlation between the real-model and randomized-model attribution maps implies. (2 pts)
3. State the symbol grounding problem precisely, in Harnad's own terms. (2 pts)
4. Why is the symbol grounding problem treated as a live research question for systems with
   learned, high-dimensional internal representations, rather than a solved historical
   curiosity? (2 pts)
5. True or false, with one sentence of justification: passing the randomization sanity check is
   sufficient evidence that an attribution method reflects a model's genuine computational
   mechanism. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
