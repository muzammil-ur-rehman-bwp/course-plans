# Quiz 2 — Multi-Armed & Contextual Bandits (Week 5)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the Hoeffding-bound argument used to justify UCB1's confidence radius
   √(2 ln t / nᵢ(t)). (2 pts)
2. State UCB1's expected-regret growth rate in terms of K, T, and the suboptimality gap Δ, and
   say in one sentence why this growth is sublinear in T. (2 pts)
3. Describe, in 2–3 sentences, how Thompson sampling achieves exploration without computing an
   explicit confidence bound. (2 pts)
4. In a contextual bandit, what does the learner observe before choosing an arm each round, and
   against what benchmark policy is its regret measured (as opposed to the Week 3 bandit's)? (2 pts)
5. True or false, with one sentence of justification: a contextual bandit requires a transition
   model P(s'|s,a) the way a full MDP does. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
