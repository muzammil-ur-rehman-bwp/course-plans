# Quiz 1 — Online Learning & Regret Minimization (Week 3)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the formal online-learning protocol and the definition of regret precisely. (2 pts)
2. Write the multiplicative-weights regret bound Regret_T ≤ ηT + (ln N)/η, and state the
   optimized bound obtained by choosing η as a function of T and N. (2 pts)
3. In the multiplicative-weights potential-function derivation, why must η be kept ≤ 1/2? (2 pts)
4. True or false, with one sentence of justification: a smaller η always gives lower regret,
   regardless of T. (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "If one expert is secretly perfect (zero loss every round), multiplicative
   weights' regret against that expert is zero." Identify what is wrong with this claim. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
