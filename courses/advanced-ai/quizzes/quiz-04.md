# Quiz 4 — AI Safety & Alignment (Week 10)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Define specification gaming, and state the Goodhart's-law-style intuition behind why it
   occurs. (2 pts)
2. Summarize the CoastRunners example in one sentence, and state which alignment sub-problem —
   outer or inner — it illustrates. (2 pts)
3. State the outer-alignment vs. inner-alignment distinction precisely. (2 pts)
4. Why can an inner-alignment failure (a misaligned mesa-objective) remain undetected throughout
   training, unlike an outer-alignment failure? (2 pts)
5. In 1–2 sentences, describe how reward modeling learns a reward function from pairwise human
   preferences rather than a hand-specified score, and name one concrete limitation it inherits.
   (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
