# Quiz 3 — Multi-Agent RL & Nash-Equilibrium Computation (Week 7)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. Why does independent Q-learning by multiple simultaneously-learning agents violate the
   stationarity assumption single-agent Q-learning's convergence proof relies on? (2 pts)
2. What is the key structural difference between an independent learner's Q(a) and a
   joint-action learner's Q(a, b)? (2 pts)
3. For two-player zero-sum games, which theorem guarantees a Nash equilibrium is computable in
   polynomial time, and via what computational method? (2 pts)
4. What does "PPAD-complete" mean, and why is Nash-equilibrium computation for general-sum games
   believed to admit no polynomial-time algorithm despite not being NP-hard? (2 pts)
5. True or false, with one sentence of justification: every correlated equilibrium is also a
   Nash equilibrium. (2 pts)

before the quiz).**
