# Quiz 6 — The Chinese Room & Research Methods (Week 14)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the Chinese Room argument's conclusion in one sentence, and identify which premise the
   Systems Reply targets. (2 pts)
2. What does the Robot Reply argue, and how does it connect back to the symbol grounding
   problem? (2 pts)
3. A student's draft problem statement reads "I want to study bandit algorithms." Explain, in
   1–2 sentences, why this fails the Week 13 precision test, and briefly rewrite it so it
   passes. (2 pts)
4. Name the three things a postgraduate-level related-work survey must do that a weaker survey
   often fails at. (2 pts)
5. Name the three things a rigorous feasibility argument must state explicitly. (2 pts)

**Answer key available to instructors in the course LMS gradebook (not distributed to students
before the quiz).**
