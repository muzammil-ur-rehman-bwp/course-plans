# Week 13 Lecture Plan — Advanced Artificial Intelligence (Post Graduate)
## Topic: Research Methods at the Postgraduate Level

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour research seminar/lab (workshop format)

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Write a precise, falsifiable problem statement. (*Apply*)
2. Apply postgraduate related-work-survey standards to 5+ candidate papers. (*Apply, Analyze*)
3. Evaluate how postgraduate proposal evaluation differs from the graduate course's
   literature-review-plus-experiment standard. (*Evaluate*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Problem statements | What makes a statement precise and falsifiable vs. vague |
| 0:20–0:50 | Related-work survey standards | Representing papers accurately; relating them to each other; motivating a gap |
| 0:50–1:20 | Feasibility arguments | What must be true, main risk, why the risk is manageable |
| 1:20–1:50 | Graduate vs. postgraduate evaluation | Contrast: reproduced-experiment standard vs. proposal standard |
| 1:50–2:00 | Logistics | Paper Critique & Presentation assignment details |

### Materials/Equipment
- Slides: "Scoping a Research Proposal"
- Handouts: problem-statement checklist; related-work-survey rubric template

### Formative Check (in-class)
Critique a deliberately vague sample problem statement ("I want to study bandit algorithms") and
rewrite it to be precise and falsifiable.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 13: draft a problem statement and annotate 5+ candidate related-work papers (see
`lab-manuals/lab-13.md`). **Paper Critique & Presentation assignment assigned.**
