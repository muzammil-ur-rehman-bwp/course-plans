# Week 14 Lecture Plan — Advanced Artificial Intelligence (Post Graduate)
## Topic: Current Frontier Topics Survey

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour research seminar/lab

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Summarize 2–3 currently active AI research directions relevant to this course's pillars.
   (*Understand*)
2. Evaluate how each surveyed direction builds on this course's foundations and what it leaves
   open. (*Evaluate*)
3. Apply the Week 13 problem-statement skill to a current paper, as direct capstone preparation.
   (*Apply*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Framing | Explicit flag: this is a fast-moving area; the skill, not the specific facts, is what is graded |
| 0:10–1:00 | Frontier direction 1 & 2 | Instructor-selected current directions (e.g., scalable oversight maturing, mechanistic-interpretability advances), each linked back to Weeks 5–10 |
| 1:00–1:35 | Frontier direction 3 (optional) | A third direction if time and instructor selection allow |
| 1:35–1:55 | Problem-statement practice | Extract a precise problem statement from one surveyed paper |
| 1:55–2:00 | Synthesis | Transition to Week 15's proposal work session |

### Materials/Equipment
- Slides: "Current Frontier Topics" (refreshed per offering)
- Instructor-selected current papers/surveys

### Formative Check (in-class)
For one surveyed direction, state which of this course's pillars it extends and name one
specific open question it leaves unresolved.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 14: a frontier-topic mini-investigation mapping one current paper onto course concepts (see
`lab-manuals/lab-14.md`).
