# Week 15 Lecture Plan — Advanced Artificial Intelligence (Post Graduate)
## Topic: Research Proposal Work Session

**Duration:** 2 hours structured work session + 3 hour research seminar/lab (peer-review workshop)

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Produce a complete capstone proposal draft (problem statement, survey, approach, feasibility
   argument). (*Create*)
2. Give structured, committee-style critical feedback on a peer's proposal draft. (*Evaluate*)
3. Revise a proposal draft in direct response to specific feedback. (*Apply, Create*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Logistics | Deliverables and deadlines for Weeks 15–16 |
| 0:15–1:15 | Guided drafting time | Instructor circulates; targeted one-on-one feedback |
| 1:15–1:45 | Peer feedback round 1 | Structured committee-style critique in pairs/triads |
| 1:45–2:00 | Revision planning | Each student lists concrete revisions to make before the Week 15 deliverable |

### Materials/Equipment
- Peer-feedback worksheet (thesis-committee-style questions)
- Students' own proposal drafts

### Formative Check (in-class)
Each student states, in one sentence, the single weakest part of their current draft and their
specific plan to fix it.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 15: structured peer-review workshop on capstone proposal drafts (see
`lab-manuals/lab-15.md`). **Deliverable:** capstone written-proposal draft due.
