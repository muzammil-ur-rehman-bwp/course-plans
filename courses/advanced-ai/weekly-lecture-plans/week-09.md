# Week 9 Lecture Plan — Advanced Artificial Intelligence (Post Graduate)
## Topic: Midterm Exam; AI Safety and Alignment II

**Duration:** 2 hours (1 hour exam + 1 hour lecture) + 3 hour research seminar/lab

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Demonstrate qualifying-exam-level mastery of Weeks 1–8. (*Remember, Understand, Apply,
   Analyze*)
2. Describe current alignment-research directions — reward modeling, scalable oversight,
   interpretability-as-safety — at a grounded, technical level. (*Understand, Analyze*)
3. Evaluate the strengths and limitations of reward modeling as an outer-alignment approach.
   (*Evaluate*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–1:00 | Midterm Exam | Closed-book, qualifying-exam style, Weeks 1–8 |
| 1:00–1:25 | Reward modeling | Learning a reward function from preference comparisons; why this narrows (but does not close) the outer-alignment gap |
| 1:25–1:50 | Scalable oversight | General mechanism of debate-/amplification-style approaches; why evaluation-difficulty is the core problem they target |
| 1:50–2:00 | Interpretability as a safety tool | Framing preview for Week 10 |

### Materials/Equipment
- Midterm exam booklet/online exam
- Slides: "AI Safety II: Reward Modeling & Scalable Oversight"

### Formative Check (in-class)
State one concrete limitation of reward modeling that is inherited directly from its training
data (preference labels), rather than from the learning algorithm used to fit it.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 9: fit a small preference-based reward model via logistic regression (see
`lab-manuals/lab-09.md`).
