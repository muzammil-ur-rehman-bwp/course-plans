# Week 16 Lecture Plan — Advanced Artificial Intelligence (Post Graduate)
## Topic: Capstone Research Proposal Presentations; Course Review

**Duration:** 2 hours presentations + 3 hour presentations/review session (no lab this week)

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Defend a complete research proposal under committee-style questioning. (*Evaluate, Create*)
2. Critically evaluate peers' proposals via substantive Q&A. (*Evaluate*)
3. Synthesize the course's four pillars into a coherent map connecting to future thesis work.
   (*Understand, Evaluate*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–1:40 | Proposal defenses | Each student presents (10–12 min) + qualifying-exam-style Q&A (5–8 min) |
| 1:40–2:00 | Course review | Recap of the four-pillar map and its connection to the sibling postgraduate courses |
| 2:00–4:00 | Remaining defenses | Continued presentations/Q&A (research-seminar block) |
| 4:00–5:00 | Closing discussion | Where this course's foundations lead — thesis/dissertation trajectories |
