# Week 1 Lecture Plan — Advanced Artificial Intelligence (Post Graduate)
## Topic: Postgraduate Overview — The AI Research Frontier

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour research seminar/lab

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Recall the graduate-AI formalism this course assumes (advanced search, game theory, SAT/SMT,
   planning complexity, MDPs/POMDPs/RL, AI complexity, research methods). (*Remember*)
2. Map this course's research-frontier pillars (regret/bandits, multi-agent RL, algorithmic game
   theory, AI safety/alignment, interpretability, foundational debates) onto the broader AI
   landscape, and explain how this course's scope is disjoint from the sibling postgraduate
   courses. (*Understand*)
3. State, in outline, what a research proposal's four components (problem statement, related-
   work survey, proposed approach, feasibility argument) are. (*Understand*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Course intro | Syllabus walkthrough, grading, capstone-proposal preview |
| 0:15–0:45 | Assumed-foundations review | Rapid-fire recap checklist: advanced search, game theory, SAT, planning, MDPs/RL, complexity — flagged explicitly as assumed, not re-taught |
| 0:45–1:20 | Research-frontier map | Slide walkthrough: this course's four pillars and where they sit relative to the graduate course and sibling postgraduate courses |
| 1:20–1:50 | Proposal scoping, previewed | Introduce the four components of a research proposal at a high level, to frame the whole semester |
| 1:50–2:00 | Q&A | Open floor |

### Materials/Equipment
- Slides: "AI Research Frontier: Course Map & Assumed Foundations"
- Handout: self-assessment checklist against graduate-AI prerequisites
- Handout: syllabus + capstone-proposal overview

### Formative Check (in-class)
Given a short research-question prompt, identify which of this course's four pillars it belongs
to, and name one graduate-AI topic it assumes as background without re-deriving it.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 1: environment setup + research-landscape mapping and a first-draft research-interest
paragraph (see `lab-manuals/lab-01.md`). No graded assignment this week.
