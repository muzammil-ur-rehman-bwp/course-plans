# Lab Manual 15 — Capstone Peer-Review Workshop

**Duration:** 3 hours | **Prerequisite:** Week 15 lecture; a complete draft capstone proposal

## Objectives
Give and receive structured, thesis-committee-style peer feedback on complete capstone proposal
drafts, and produce a written, itemized revision plan.

## Setup
1. Bring your complete draft proposal (all four required components — see
   `assignments/capstone-proposal-guidelines.md`).
2. Form review groups of 3–4 students (instructor-assigned or self-organized).
3. Create `lab15-revision-plan.md`.

## Procedure
1. **Task A — Self-check:** before the workshop, apply the Week 15 four review questions to your
   own draft and note, honestly, where you expect it to be weakest.
2. **Task B — Peer review (as reviewer):** for each other draft in your group, answer all four
   review questions in writing with specific, actionable feedback (not general impressions).
3. **Task C — Receiving feedback (as author):** collect the written feedback from each reviewer in
   your group; ask clarifying questions in the spirit of a real committee Q&A.
4. **Task D — Revision plan:** in `lab15-revision-plan.md`, for every piece of feedback received,
   state whether you agree, what exactly you will change and where (if you agree), or why you
   disagree (if you do not).

## Expected Output
Written peer-review feedback given to each group member (Task B) and a complete, itemized
revision plan for your own proposal (Task D).

## Submission
Submit `lab15-revision-plan.md`, and the peer-review feedback you gave to your group members, via
the course submission system by the end of the lab session. **Also due this week:** the complete
draft capstone proposal itself, and the Paper Critique & Presentation (presented in-class).
