# Lab Manual 13 — Critiquing a Systems Paper and Starting the Capstone Survey

**Duration:** 3 hours | **Prerequisite:** Week 13 lecture

## Objectives
Critique a short DL systems-and-methods paper excerpt using the structured framework; begin the
capstone literature search and draft a precision-tested problem statement.

## Setup
1. Obtain the instructor-provided paper excerpt.
2. Create `lab13-critique.md` and `lab13-capstone-start.md`.

## Procedure
1. **Task A — Structured critique:** using the Week 13 framework, state the excerpt's precise
   claim (with configuration details), assess baseline/ablation fairness, and identify one
   concrete reproducibility concern.
2. **Task B — Benchmark-culture check:** identify whether the excerpt shows any sign of
   leaderboard-chasing, benchmark saturation/contamination risk, or a benchmark-vs-deployment gap,
   or explicitly note if none is apparent and why.
3. **Task C — Literature search:** identify 3–5 candidate papers connected to your tentative
   capstone topic area (one of this course's eight topics, or adjacent territory).
4. **Task D — Problem-statement draft:** draft a one-paragraph, falsifiable problem statement for
   your candidate capstone topic, and subject it to the Week 13 precision test via peer feedback
   from at least one classmate; record the feedback received and your response to it.

## Expected Output
Two written documents (`lab13-critique.md` for Tasks A–B, `lab13-capstone-start.md` for Tasks
C–D).

## Submission
Submit both documents via the course submission system by the end of the lab session.
