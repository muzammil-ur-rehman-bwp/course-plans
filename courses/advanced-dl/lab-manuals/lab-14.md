# Lab Manual 14 — Open Problems Critical Writing

**Duration:** 3 hours | **Prerequisite:** Week 14 lecture

## Objectives
Write a structured statement of what is known/unknown for one surveyed open problem, and relate
it to your own capstone direction.

## Setup
1. Review the Week 14 lecture content's surveyed open problems.
2. Create `lab14.md`.

## Procedure
1. **Task A — Selection:** choose one surveyed open problem (MoE load balancing, ICL-mechanism
   generality, DPO's Bradley-Terry robustness, or the instructor-refreshed fourth).
2. **Task B — Known/unknown statement:** write what is currently known about your chosen problem,
   with its evidentiary basis, in 100–150 words.
3. **Task C — Unknown and obstruction:** write what remains unknown, stated precisely, and name
   the specific technical obstruction that makes it genuinely open (not merely unattempted), in
   100–150 words.
4. **Task D — Capstone relation:** state, in 100–150 words, how this open question relates (or
   does not relate) to your own emerging capstone interest, and whether engaging with it changes
   your current problem-statement draft from Lab 13.

## Expected Output
A single written document (`lab14.md`) addressing Tasks A–D, totaling 250–350 words for B–D
combined per the Week 14 lecture's exercise.

## Submission
Submit `lab14.md` via the course submission system by the end of the lab session.
