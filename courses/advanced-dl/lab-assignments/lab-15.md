# Lab Assignment 15 — Capstone Peer-Review Workshop (Graded)

**Weight:** part of the weekly Lab Work component (10% of course grade, averaged across all labs).

## Deliverable
Submit `lab15-revision-plan.md` and the written peer-review feedback you gave to your group
members, per `lab-manuals/lab-15.md`.

## Grading Rubric
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Task A complete (honest, specific self-check against the four review questions) | 1 |
| Task B complete (specific, actionable feedback given to each group member on all four questions) | 4 |
| Task C evidenced (clarifying questions/engagement during the workshop, reflected in the revision plan's context) | 1 |
| Task D complete (itemized, concrete revision plan addressing every piece of feedback received) | 4 |
| **Total** | **10** |

## Notes
- This is the last graded lab of the semester; its quality directly affects how well-prepared each
  student is for the Week 16 capstone defense, worth 40% of the course grade.
- Peer-review feedback is graded on specificity and actionability, not on how positive or
  negative it is.
