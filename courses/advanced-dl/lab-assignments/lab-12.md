# Lab Assignment 12 — Critiquing a Current Fast-Sampler Paper (Graded)

**Weight:** part of the weekly Lab Work component (10% of course grade, averaged across all labs).

## Deliverable
Submit `lab12-critique.md` (required) containing Tasks A–C from `lab-manuals/lab-12.md`, and
optionally `lab12.ipynb` for Task D.

## Grading Rubric
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Task A correct (precise claim extraction, exact step counts/benchmarks stated) | 3 |
| Task B correct (specific, well-reasoned baseline-fairness assessment) | 4 |
| Task C correct (specific, non-generic caution flag identified) | 3 |
| Task D (optional bonus: working consistency-style extension with a sound comparison) | up to +2 |
| **Total** | **10 (+2 bonus)** |

## Notes
- This lab is graded on the precision and specificity of the critique, not on whether the chosen
  paper's method turns out to be impressive or underwhelming.
- The Task D bonus cannot bring a submission above 10 total labs-component points for this lab,
  but may offset a lower score on another lab within the semester average at instructor
  discretion.
