# Quiz 6 — Frontier Generative-Modeling Survey and Research Methods (Week 14)

**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Format:** 5 short-answer/code questions | **Weight:** part of
Quizzes (10%, best 5 of 6 counted)

1. State the consistency-model mechanism's defining property, and explain why it enables one- or
   few-step sampling. (2 pts)
2. Explain, in 2–3 sentences, how progressive/step distillation reuses Week 8's teacher-student
   distillation framing, naming what plays the role of "teacher" and "student." (2 pts)
3. Name two specific benchmark-culture failure modes discussed in Week 13, and define each in one
   sentence. (2 pts)
4. State, in one sentence, why compute cost is itself a reproducibility barrier specific to
   large-scale deep learning research. (2 pts)
5. A colleague claims: "Since this week's fast-sampler survey is explicitly flagged as fast-
   moving, its content isn't worth learning carefully." Identify precisely what is wrong with
   this claim. (2 pts)
