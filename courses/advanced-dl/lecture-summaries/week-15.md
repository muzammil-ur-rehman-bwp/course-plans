# Week 15 Summary — Research Proposal Work Session

**Key takeaways:**
- The four review questions (precise/open problem statement; survey accuracy/synthesis; genuine
  novelty of approach; honest feasibility argument) structure both peer review and self-review.
- A feasibility argument that only lists reasons an approach will work has not met the required
  standard — the single most likely failure mode must be named honestly.
- A written, itemized revision plan is the direct bridge from this week's feedback to the Week 16
  final submission.

**You should now be able to:** give and receive structured, specific, committee-style peer
feedback on a complete proposal draft; produce an itemized revision plan in response.

**Next week:** Capstone research-proposal presentations, defended in a qualifying-exam/thesis-
proposal-defense format, followed by course review.
