# Week 13 Summary — Research Methods for Applied Deep Learning Research

**Key takeaways:**
- Reading a systems-and-methods DL paper critically means extracting the precise claim and
  configuration, assessing baseline/ablation fairness, and identifying concrete reproducibility
  concerns.
- Benchmark culture (leaderboard-chasing, saturation/contamination, the benchmark-vs-deployment
  gap) and DL-specific reproducibility obstacles (compute cost as a barrier, hyperparameter/
  hardware sensitivity) are named precisely, not treated as vague worries.
- The same precision test used across the postgraduate sequence applies to drafting a capstone
  problem statement: a reader should know, from the statement alone, what evidence would resolve
  it.

**You should now be able to:** critique a DL systems-and-methods paper using the structured
framework; identify benchmark-culture and reproducibility risks; draft and precision-test a
capstone problem statement.
