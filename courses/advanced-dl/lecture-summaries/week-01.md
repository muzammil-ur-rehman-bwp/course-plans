# Week 1 Summary — The Frontier Deep-Learning Landscape

**Key takeaways:**
- This course assumes the graduate *Deep Learning* course's Transformer-from-scratch, advanced
  CNNs, contrastive learning, DDPM diffusion, GNNs, deep RL, and large-scale training basics as
  firm background — none of it is re-taught.
- The three sibling postgraduate courses own: theoretical scaling laws and NN theory (ANN);
  regret/multi-agent/game theory/alignment-as-research-agenda (AI); statistical learning theory
  (ML). This course owns frontier DL engineering/research: SDE diffusion, MoE, ICL mechanics,
  RLHF/DPO engineering, efficient inference, NAS, systems-view scaling.
- Scaling laws and alignment each have a "theory vs. systems/engineering" or
  "research-agenda vs. engineering" split across this course and a sibling — know which side is
  which.

**You should now be able to:** state precisely which graduate-course results this course assumes;
map a frontier-DL paper title to the correct course and week; state the two key scoping
boundaries (scaling laws, alignment) precisely.
