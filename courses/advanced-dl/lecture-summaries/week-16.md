# Week 16 Summary — Capstone Presentations and Course Review

**Key takeaways:**
- Capstone proposals are defended in a qualifying-exam/thesis-proposal-defense format: problem
  statement, related work, proposed approach, feasibility argument/preliminary results,
  anticipated risks, committee-style Q&A.
- The course map runs SDE diffusion → advanced diffusion → MoE → ICL mechanics → RLHF/DPO →
  efficient inference → NAS → systems-view scaling → frontier survey → research methods → open
  problems → capstone, each topic extending a specific graduate-course result.
- This course's scoping boundaries against the three sibling postgraduate courses (theoretical NN
  theory, alignment-as-research-agenda, statistical learning theory) remain the right place to
  look for deeper follow-up questions a capstone might eventually raise.

**You should now be able to:** defend a complete research proposal under committee-style
questioning; recall and connect the full course map; locate where this course's frontier-
engineering foundation leads in the broader postgraduate sequence and in current DL research.
