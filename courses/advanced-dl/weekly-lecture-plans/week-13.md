# Week 13 Lecture Plan — Advanced Deep Learning (Post Graduate)
## Topic: Research Methods for Applied Deep Learning Research

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour research seminar/lab

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Critique a systems-and-methods DL paper's claim, evidence, baselines/ablations, and
   reproducibility. (*Analyze*)
2. Identify benchmark-culture and reproducibility risks specific to large-scale DL research.
   (*Analyze, Evaluate*)
3. Draft a precise, falsifiable problem statement for a candidate capstone topic. (*Create*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | Reading a systems paper | The claim/evidence/baselines-ablations/reproducibility framework |
| 0:30–1:00 | Benchmark culture | Leaderboard-chasing, saturation/contamination, benchmark-vs-deployment gap |
| 1:00–1:20 | Reproducibility in DL specifically | Compute cost as a reproduction barrier; hyperparameter/hardware sensitivity |
| 1:20–1:50 | Capstone work time | Topic finalization, literature search (3–5 candidate papers) |
| 1:50–2:00 | Problem-statement drafting | The precision test, applied to each student's candidate topic |

### Materials/Equipment
- Instructor-provided guidance handout on critiquing DL systems-and-methods papers
- Suggested-topics list for the Paper Critique & Presentation assignment

### Formative Check (in-class)
Given a short systems-paper excerpt, state its central claim, one baseline-fairness concern, and
one concrete reproducibility concern, in one sentence each.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 13 (critical-writing, capstone work): critique a short DL systems-paper excerpt and begin the
capstone literature search and problem-statement draft (see `lab-manuals/lab-13.md`). Paper
Critique & Presentation assignment assigned this week (due Week 15).
