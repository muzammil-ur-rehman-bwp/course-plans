# Week 15 Lecture Plan — Advanced Deep Learning (Post Graduate)
## Topic: Research Proposal Work Session

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture (workshop format) + 3 hour research seminar/lab

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Give structured, specific, thesis-committee-style peer feedback on a complete proposal draft.
   (*Evaluate*)
2. Revise a proposal draft in direct response to itemized feedback. (*Create*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Workshop framing | The four review questions: precise/open problem statement? survey synthesizes and motivates the gap? approach genuinely distinguishable? feasibility argument honest about risk? |
| 0:15–1:15 | Peer-review workshop | Structured small-group review of complete draft proposals |
| 1:15–1:45 | Revision planning | Each student drafts a written, itemized revision plan from feedback received |
| 1:45–2:00 | Logistics | Week 16 defense format and timing reminder |

### Materials/Equipment
- Structured peer-review worksheet (four review questions above)
- Students' own complete draft proposals

### Formative Check (in-class)
Using the four review questions, each reviewer writes one specific, actionable piece of feedback
per question for the proposal they are reviewing — not a general impression.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 15: structured peer-review workshop and revision-plan drafting (see `lab-manuals/lab-15.md`).
**Deliverable this week:** draft capstone proposal due (all four required components); Paper
Critique & Presentation due, presented in-class.
