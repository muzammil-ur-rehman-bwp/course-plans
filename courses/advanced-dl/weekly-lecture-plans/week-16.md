# Week 16 Lecture Plan — Advanced Deep Learning (Post Graduate)
## Topic: Capstone Research Proposal Presentations + Course Review

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture (presentations) + 3 hour research seminar/lab (presentations
continued / course review)

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Deliver and defend a complete research proposal in a qualifying-exam/thesis-proposal-defense
   format under committee-style questioning. (*Evaluate, Create*)
2. Recall and connect the full course map, and situate it against the sibling postgraduate
   courses. (*Remember, Understand*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–1:40 | Capstone defenses | Each student presents (12–15 min) + committee-style Q&A |
| 1:40–2:00 | Course review | Recap of the full course map; connections to the sibling postgraduate courses |

### Materials/Equipment
- Presentation system; `presentations/capstone-presentation-template.md`
- Course-review slide deck

recap discussion.
