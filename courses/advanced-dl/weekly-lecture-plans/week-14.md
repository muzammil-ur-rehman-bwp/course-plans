# Week 14 Lecture Plan — Advanced Deep Learning (Post Graduate)
## Topic: Current Open Problems Survey

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour research seminar/lab

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. State precisely what is known and not known for each surveyed open problem. (*Evaluate*)
2. Relate at least one surveyed open question to the student's own emerging capstone interest.
   (*Evaluate, Create*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Framing | Explicit "fast-moving, refreshed each offering" flag |
| 0:15–1:00 | Open problem 1 | MoE load-balancing without an auxiliary loss — current state, what's missing |
| 1:00–1:30 | Open problem 2 | Generality limits of the induction-heads/implicit-GD picture of ICL |
| 1:30–1:55 | Open problem 3 | DPO's robustness to Bradley-Terry violations |
| 1:55–2:00 | Synthesis | Connecting surveyed questions to students' capstone interests |

### Materials/Equipment
- Instructor-curated current papers/preprints (refreshed each offering)
- Slides: "Current Open Problems Survey"

### Formative Check (in-class)
For one surveyed open question, state precisely what would count as progress toward resolving it,
and what the strongest current partial answer actually establishes.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 14 (critical-writing): write a structured statement of what is known/not known for one
surveyed open problem, and relate it to your own capstone direction (see `lab-manuals/lab-14.md`).
Quiz 6 this week (Weeks 12–13 content).
