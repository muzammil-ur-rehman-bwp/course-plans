# Week 1 Lecture Plan — Advanced Deep Learning (Post Graduate)
## Topic: Course Overview — The Frontier Deep-Learning Landscape

**Duration:** 2 hours lecture + 3 hour research seminar/lab

### Learning Objectives (Bloom's Level)
1. Recall, at a checklist level, the graduate *Deep Learning* course's architectures/techniques
   this course assumes without re-teaching. (*Remember*)
2. State precisely how this course's territory is scoped against the three sibling postgraduate
   courses (*Advanced Artificial Neural Network*, *Advanced Artificial Intelligence*, *Advanced
   Machine Learning*). (*Understand*)
3. Map a given frontier-DL paper title to the week of this course it belongs to. (*Understand*)

### Lecture Structure
| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Course framing | Why this is the terminal course in the DL sequence; the four sibling postgraduate courses and how they partition the frontier-research space |
| 0:20–0:50 | Assumed foundations, checklist pass | Rapid recall pass over Transformer-from-scratch, advanced CNNs, contrastive learning, DDPM, GNNs, deep RL, large-scale training basics — no re-teaching, only a fluency self-check |
| 0:50–1:20 | This course's landscape | SDE diffusion, advanced diffusion, MoE, ICL mechanics, RLHF/DPO, efficient inference, NAS, systems-view scaling, frontier survey, research methods, capstone |
| 1:20–1:50 | Scoping vs. siblings | Explicit boundary statements: scaling laws (theory vs. systems), alignment (research-agenda vs. engineering), no-overlap confirmation with Advanced ML |
| 1:50–2:00 | Research-proposal preview | One-slide sketch of problem statement/survey/approach/feasibility argument, full treatment in Week 13 |

### Materials/Equipment
- Slides: "Advanced Deep Learning: Course Overview"
- Instructor-provided one-page frontier-landscape map handout
- Live environment-check notebook (Jupyter/Colab, PyTorch version check)

### Formative Check (in-class)
Given five paper titles (one per sibling-course territory plus one genuinely belonging to this
course), each student states which course — this one or a sibling — would own it, and why, in one
sentence per title.

### Link to Lab/Assessment
Lab 1: environment setup, prerequisite self-assessment, and a short landscape-mapping exercise
(see `lab-manuals/lab-01.md`).
